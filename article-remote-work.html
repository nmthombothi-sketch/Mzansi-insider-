How to Get Remote Work from South Africa (and Earn in Forex)

South Africans are perfectly positioned for global remote work — thanks to strong business English, a useful GMT+2 time zone, and the powerful rand advantage. Here's exactly how to break in.

By Thabo Mokoena, Career & Finance Analyst | Updated: September 7, 2026

Why South Africans Have an Edge

South African remote workers are highly sought after globally for their strong business English and a GMT+2 time zone that perfectly bridges European and American working hours. Furthermore, because you can earn in a stronger currency (USD, GBP, or EUR) while living expenses remain in rands, the financial maths is incredibly compelling.

Remote Work Roadmap at a Glance

Step Key Action
1. Skill Acquisition Master a portable digital skill (e.g., coding, data, design, copywriting).
2. Portfolio Building Create a GitHub, Behance, or personal website showcasing real-world projects.
3. Platform Setup Optimize profiles on Upwork, Toptal, We Work Remotely, and LinkedIn.
4. Financial Compliance Set up a forex-friendly bank account (e.g., Wise, Payoneer) and register for provisional tax if applicable.

Build an In-Demand Skill

Remote work rewards portable, digital skills that can be delivered asynchronously or via video call. Software development, data analysis, UX/UI design, digital marketing, technical writing, and high-level virtual assistance are consistently in demand. Where to Find Remote Roles

Global freelance platforms (Upwork, Toptal, Fiverr) and dedicated remote job boards (We Work Remotely, Remote OK, FlexJobs) list thousands of roles. Additionally, many international companies now hire directly in South Africa through Employer of Record (EOR) services like Deel or Oyster, offering full-time employment with foreign benefits.

Prove Your Skills

Remote employers can't watch you work, so they hire based on demonstrated ability. A robust portfolio, an active GitHub repository, or a track record of measurable results matters far more than formal credentials. Start with smaller contracts to build glowing reviews and a solid reputation.

Mind the Practicalities

Reliable fibre internet, a professional home office setup, and disciplined, proactive communication are non-negotiable. Crucially, remember that forex income earned while you are a South African tax resident is still taxable by SARS. Keep meticulous records and understand your provisional tax obligations.

Consider a Side Hustle First

You don't have to quit your day job immediately. Many successful remote careers start as side hustles — such as freelance writing, virtual assistance, or online tutoring — that gradually grow into a full-time, location-independent income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can South Africans work remotely for foreign companies?
Yes. South Africans are sought after for strong business English and a GMT+2 time zone, and are increasingly hired through global platforms and employer-of-record services.
What skills are best for remote work?
Portable digital skills: software development, data analysis, design, digital marketing, writing, and virtual assistance are all in high demand for remote roles.
Why is remote work for foreign companies attractive?
You can earn in a stronger currency like dollars or euros while spending in rands, creating a powerful wealth-building advantage for skilled South Africans.
Is foreign remote income taxable in South Africa?
Yes. Income earned from foreign remote work is still taxable in South Africa if you are a tax resident, so it's important to keep records and understand your SARS obligations.
