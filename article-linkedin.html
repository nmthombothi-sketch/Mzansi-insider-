How to Build a LinkedIn Profile That Gets You Hired

Recruiters search LinkedIn constantly. A well-optimised profile turns you from invisible to in-demand. Here's exactly how to build one.

By Thabo Mokoena, Career & Finance Analyst | Updated: September 7, 2026

Why LinkedIn Matters

Many South African recruiters and hiring managers source candidates directly on LinkedIn, often before a job is even formally advertised. A strong, search-optimised profile means opportunities come to you, rather than you constantly chasing them.

LinkedIn Optimisation Checklist at a Glance

Profile Section Optimisation Goal
Headline Include target job title + core skills + unique value proposition.
About Section Write in the first person. Tell a genuine story about your expertise and goals.
Experience Focus on quantified achievements (numbers, results, impact), not just duties.
Skills & Keywords Sprinkle industry-specific keywords naturally to beat the recruiter search algorithm.

Nail Your Headline

Your headline is the most-read part of your profile. Don't just list your current job title — describe what you do and the specific value you bring, using the exact keywords recruiters are searching for (e.g., "Data Analyst | Python, SQL & Tableau | Turning Data into Actionable Business Insights").

Write a Real "About" Section

Write your About section in the first person, telling a short, genuine story: what you do, what you're exceptionally good at, and what kind of roles you're looking for. Make it human and engaging, not a dry list of corporate buzzwords.

"Recruiters search by keyword — if your profile doesn't contain the terms they're searching for, you simply don't appear in their results, no matter how good you are."

Use Keywords Strategically

Sprinkle the skills, tools, and industry terms relevant to your target field naturally throughout your headline, About section, and experience descriptions. This strategic keyword placement is what makes you findable in recruiter searches.

Show, Don't Just Tell

Quantify your achievements in your experience section — use numbers, percentages, and clear results to demonstrate your impact. Add relevant skills to your profile, request recommendations from former colleagues, and ensure your profile photo is professional, well-lit, and friendly.

Network with Intent

Don't just collect connections. Connect with people in your industry, engage with their posts thoughtfully, and share the occasional useful insight or article. Consistent, genuine visibility compounds into real job opportunities over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I optimise my LinkedIn profile?
Write a value-focused headline with keywords, a genuine first-person About section, quantified achievements, relevant skills, a professional photo, and network with intent.
Why do keywords matter on LinkedIn?
Recruiters search LinkedIn by keyword. If your profile doesn't contain the terms they search for, you won't appear in their results, regardless of your ability.
What makes a good LinkedIn headline?
One that describes what you do and the value you bring, using keywords recruiters search for — not just your job title.
Does networking on LinkedIn help with jobs?
Yes. Connecting with people in your industry, engaging thoughtfully, and sharing useful insights builds visibility, which compounds into opportunities over time.
