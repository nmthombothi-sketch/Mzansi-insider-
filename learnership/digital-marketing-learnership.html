About This Learnership

The Digital Marketing Learnership, facilitated through the MICT SETA, is a highly practical 12-month programme designed to equip young South Africans with in-demand skills in social media management, digital advertising, search engine optimization (SEO), and online content creation.

As businesses of all sizes increasingly shift their budgets to online channels, the demand for skilled digital marketers continues to surge. This learnership provides a unique dual advantage: it prepares you for full-time corporate employment while also giving you the exact skills needed to launch a lucrative freelance career or side hustle.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Technical Skills: Basic computer literacy, familiarity with social media platforms, and an aptitude for learning new digital tools.
  • Attributes: Creative, detail-oriented, and possessing strong written communication skills.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any creative, writing, or social media experience
  • ✅ A portfolio or links to any social media pages, blogs, or content you have created (highly recommended)
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your passion for digital marketing

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and highly marketable education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R5,000 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited MICT SETA NQF Level 5 qualification in Digital Marketing, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training managing real social media campaigns, analyzing web traffic, and creating engaging digital content.
  • Freelance Readiness: The exact skill set needed to confidently pitch your services to small businesses as a freelance social media manager or content creator.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the digital marketing learnership pool requires proving you already have a knack for the digital world:

  • Build a Mini Portfolio: Even if it's just a personal Instagram page you grew, a blog you wrote, or Canva designs you made, show it! Employers love to see practical proof of your skills.
  • Stay Updated on Trends: Mention current digital marketing trends (like short-form video, AI in marketing, or specific platform algorithms) in your motivational letter to show you are genuinely passionate.
  • Highlight Transferable Skills: If you've done volunteer work, school projects, or informal gigs involving writing, design, or organizing, highlight them on your CV.
  • Proofread Everything: Digital marketing requires strong written communication. Ensure your CV, email, and motivational letter are completely free of spelling or grammatical errors.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 5 Digital Marketing learnership opens several dynamic and flexible career paths:

  • Social Media Coordinator: Managing brand presence, creating content calendars, and engaging with online communities.
  • Digital Marketing Assistant: Supporting marketing managers with email campaigns, SEO basics, and analytics reporting.
  • Content Creator or Copywriter: Specializing in writing engaging blog posts, website copy, or producing video content.
  • Paid Ads / Performance Marketing Assistant: Learning to manage and optimize paid advertising campaigns on platforms like Meta or Google.
  • Freelance Digital Marketer: Leveraging your accredited skills and portfolio to offer social media management or content creation services to small businesses on a contract basis.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the digital marketing learnership pay? +
It pays a monthly stipend of around R5,000 over a 12-month period, plus you earn a recognized MICT SETA NQF Level 5 digital marketing qualification.
Can digital marketing skills be used freelance? +
Yes. Social media management, content creation, and paid ads skills are highly sought after for freelance and side-hustle income, offering great flexibility.
Who can apply for the digital marketing learnership? +
Unemployed South African youth (typically 18–35) with a Grade 12 / Matric certificate, computer literacy, and a strong interest in marketing, social media, and content creation.
Is digital marketing in demand? +
Yes. Digital marketing is one of the fastest-growing fields globally, with strong, consistent demand across businesses of all sizes, from startups to large corporations.
