About This Learnership

The Dis-Chem Dispensary Support Learnership is a highly practical 12-month programme designed to train young South Africans in the day-to-day operations of a retail pharmacy. Participants learn dispensary support, stock management, and exceptional customer service, leading directly to an accredited NQF Level 3 Pharmacist Assistant qualification.

As one of South Africa's leading health and beauty retailers, Dis-Chem offers learners a dynamic, fast-paced environment to gain real-world experience. This learnership serves as a perfect entry point for those looking to build a stable, long-term career in the healthcare and pharmacy sector.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate with Mathematics and a science subject (Life Sciences or Physical Science).
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 34.
  • Attributes: Reliable, friendly, detail-oriented, and possessing a genuine interest in healthcare and helping people.
  • Language: Fluent in English (both written and spoken) to handle prescriptions and customer queries accurately.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any retail, healthcare, or customer service experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your passion for pharmacy and patient care

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and specialized education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R4,500 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 3 Pharmacist Assistant qualification, recognized by the South African Pharmacy Council (SAPC).
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real Dis-Chem dispensary, learning to assist pharmacists, manage stock, and provide excellent customer service.
  • Professional Registration: Upon completion, you can register as a Pharmacist Assistant with the SAPC, making you legally qualified to work in any pharmacy in South Africa.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Dis-Chem learnership pool requires demonstrating a genuine passion for healthcare and exceptional attention to detail:

  • Highlight Science Subjects: Emphasize your performance in Life Sciences, Physical Science, or Biology on your CV. This shows you have the foundational knowledge for pharmacy.
  • Showcase Customer Service: Mention any retail, hospitality, or volunteer experience where you helped people. Pharmacy is as much about customer care as it is about science.
  • Demonstrate Attention to Detail: In your motivational letter, give an example of a time your accuracy prevented an error. Dispensing medication requires absolute precision.
  • Research Dis-Chem: Understand Dis-Chem's focus on "health, beauty, and wellness." Mentioning their commitment to accessible healthcare shows genuine interest.
  • Apply Early: Healthcare learnerships are highly competitive. Submitting a polished application early demonstrates organization and genuine interest.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 3 Dispensary Support learnership opens several stable and rewarding career paths in the healthcare sector:

  • Qualified Pharmacist Assistant: The immediate role upon completion, working under a licensed pharmacist to dispense medication and assist customers.
  • Dispensary Support Team Member: Managing the daily operations of a dispensary, training junior staff, and ensuring compliance.
  • Senior Pharmacist Assistant: With experience and further study (NQF 4), you can take on more responsibility and higher pay.
  • Retail Healthcare Team Member: Leveraging your pharmacy knowledge to work in Dis-Chem's health and beauty sections or product development.
  • Further Study to BPharm: This learnership provides a strong foundation for pursuing a Bachelor of Pharmacy (BPharm) degree if you wish to become a fully qualified pharmacist.
