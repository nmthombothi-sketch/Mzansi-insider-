About This Learnership

MTN's ICT Learnership is a premier 12-month programme designed to equip young South Africans with critical information and communications technology skills. Accredited through the MICT SETA, it covers IT support, network fundamentals, cybersecurity basics, and digital transformation.

As one of Africa's leading telecoms groups, MTN provides unparalleled exposure to enterprise-grade technology and digital infrastructure. This learnership builds capabilities that are in exceptionally high demand across South Africa's rapidly growing digital economy, offering a direct pathway into the tech sector.
