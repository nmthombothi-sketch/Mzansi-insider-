About This Learnership

Standard Bank's Learnership Programme is a highly sought-after 12-month initiative designed to develop top-tier talent across retail, commercial, and corporate banking. Participants work toward an accredited NQF Level 5 qualification in Banking while gaining hands-on experience in one of Africa's most dynamic financial institutions.

With a structured graduate pipeline, professional mentorship, and one of the highest stipends in the sector, this learnership serves as a powerful launchpad for a long-term career in financial services, whether you aim to become a personal banker, corporate advisor, or operations specialist.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate. A relevant tertiary qualification (e.g., diploma in finance or business) is often advantageous.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 30.
  • Employment Status: Must be currently unemployed and not enrolled in any full-time tertiary studies.
  • Background Checks: Must have a clear criminal record and a clean credit history (mandatory for all banking roles).

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or tertiary academic transcript
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any customer service, retail, or administrative experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in a career in banking and financial services

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you gain critical industry experience. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R7,500 per month — one of the highest training allowances offered in the South African banking sector.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 5 qualification in Banking, recognized nationwide and highly valued by employers.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real banking environment, learning about financial products, client advisory, and branch operations.
  • Career Pathway: Direct access to Standard Bank's graduate pipeline, with high-performing learners frequently fast-tracked for permanent roles.

Application Process

Step by step
1
Find the Opening: Visit the Standard Bank careers portal and search for the "Learnership 2026" listing.
2
Create a Profile: Register on the Standard Bank recruitment portal and complete your candidate profile accurately.
3
Upload Documents: Attach your CV, certified ID, academic results, and motivational letter as instructed.
4
Complete Assessments: Shortlisted candidates are usually invited to complete online numerical, verbal, and situational judgment assessments.
5
Interview & Onboarding: Successful candidates will attend a panel interview and, if selected, sign a learnership agreement to begin training.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Standard Bank learnership pool requires demonstrating professionalism and a genuine interest in financial services:

  • Highlight Customer Service: Mention any retail, hospitality, or volunteer experience where you helped people, resolved complaints, or provided excellent service.
  • Research Standard Bank's Values: Understand Standard Bank's mission of "driving Africa's growth." Mentioning this shows you align with their corporate culture.
  • Practice Assessments: Brush up on basic numerical reasoning and reading comprehension tests, as these are standard in banking recruitment.
  • Be Professional: Ensure your email address is professional, your CV is error-free, and you respond promptly to any communication from the recruitment team.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 5 Banking learnership at a major bank opens several stable and rewarding career paths:

  • Service or Sales Consultant: The most common progression, assisting clients with everyday banking needs and product recommendations.
  • Personal or Business Banker: Advising individuals or SMEs on financial products, credit, and wealth management.
  • Operations and Compliance: Supporting the backend processes that ensure the bank runs smoothly and adheres to financial regulations.
  • Corporate Banking Support: With experience, moving into roles that support large corporate clients with complex financial solutions.
  • Graduate Pipeline Progression: Top performers are often fast-tracked into Standard Bank's prestigious graduate development programmes.
