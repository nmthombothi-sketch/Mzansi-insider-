About This Learnership

Telkom's Network and Cybersecurity Learnership is a highly sought-after 12-month programme designed to equip young South Africans with critical information technology skills. Accredited through the MICT SETA, it covers network administration, IT support fundamentals, and introductory cybersecurity protocols.

As one of Africa's leading telecommunications providers, Telkom provides unparalleled exposure to enterprise-grade network infrastructure and digital security operations. This learnership builds capabilities that are in exceptionally high demand across South Africa's rapidly growing digital economy, offering a direct pathway into the tech sector.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate. Strong Mathematics and Physical Science are typically expected for the technical streams.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Technical Aptitude: Basic computer literacy, a strong interest in technology, and excellent problem-solving skills.
  • Attributes: Strong communication skills, ability to work in a team, and a proactive approach to learning new digital tools.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any IT, technical, or computer-related experience (even informal)
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your passion for technology and why you want to build a career at Telkom

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you gain critical, future-proof digital skills. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R5,500 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited MICT SETA NQF Level 5 qualification in Information Technology or Networking, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real corporate tech environment, working with enterprise networks, helpdesk systems, and digital infrastructure.
  • Career Pathway: A direct route into the tech sector, with high-performing learners frequently considered for permanent junior IT or network support roles.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Telkom learnership pool requires demonstrating a genuine passion for technology and a proactive mindset:

  • Highlight Technical Projects: Mention any personal IT projects, coding bootcamps, hardware troubleshooting, or tech-related hobbies on your CV.
  • Showcase Problem-Solving: Provide specific examples of how you've diagnosed and resolved technical or logical problems in the past.
  • Research Telkom's Tech Vision: Understand Telkom's focus on fiber expansion, 5G rollout, and digital transformation. Mentioning this shows genuine interest in the company's direction.
  • Proofread Everything: Tech roles require attention to detail. Ensure your CV and motivational letter are completely free of spelling or grammatical errors.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 5 ICT learnership at a major telecoms group opens several dynamic and high-growth career paths:

  • Network Support Technician: Assisting with the maintenance, monitoring, and configuration of enterprise network infrastructure.
  • IT Support / Help Desk Analyst: Providing first-line technical support and troubleshooting for corporate users.
  • Junior Cybersecurity Analyst: A strong foundation for specializing in high-demand fields like information security with further certifications.
  • Systems Administrator: With experience, managing servers, cloud environments, and enterprise software deployments.
  • Pathway to Cloud or Security Specialisations: The logical thinking and technical foundation gained can easily transition into specialized roles with additional upskilling.
