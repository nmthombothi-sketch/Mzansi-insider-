About This Learnership

Transnet's Supply Chain and Logistics Learnership is a premier 12-month programme accredited by the Transport Education Training Authority (TETA). It provides learners with hands-on exposure across South Africa's critical freight network, including ports, rail corridors, and pipeline operations.

With the continuous growth of e-commerce and international trade, practical logistics and supply-chain skills are in steady, high demand. This learnership offers a credible, structured pathway into both government and private-sector logistics roles, equipping you with the operational knowledge needed to keep the country's supply chains moving efficiently.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Employment Status: Must be currently unemployed and not enrolled in any full-time tertiary studies.
  • Operational Readiness: Willingness to rotate across various operational sites (e.g., ports, rail yards, depots), which may involve shift work or non-standard hours.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any organizational, administrative, or logistics-related experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in supply chain management and the transport sector

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you gain critical, industry-specific experience. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R4,700 per month to assist with transport and basic living costs during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited TETA NQF Level 4 qualification in Logistics and Supply Chain Management, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on rotational experience across Transnet's diverse business units, learning about freight coordination, inventory management, and port/rail operations.
  • Career Pathway: A direct route into the logistics sector, with high-performing learners frequently considered for permanent roles within Transnet or its broader supply chain network.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Transnet logistics learnership pool requires demonstrating reliability and a genuine interest in how supply chains operate:

  • Highlight Organizational Skills: Mention any experience coordinating events, managing schedules, or handling inventory, even in informal or volunteer settings.
  • Show Adaptability: Logistics is a fast-paced, problem-solving environment. Provide examples of how you've successfully adapted to changing circumstances or solved unexpected problems.
  • Research Transnet's Role: Understand Transnet's critical role in South Africa's economy (moving freight via rail, ports, and pipelines). Mentioning this shows you understand the bigger picture.
  • Proofread Everything: Attention to detail is crucial in logistics. Ensure your CV and motivational letter are neat, professional, and completely free of errors.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 4 Logistics learnership opens several stable and rewarding career paths in the massive transport and supply chain sector:

  • Logistics Coordinator: Managing the movement of goods, coordinating with suppliers, and ensuring timely deliveries.
  • Operations and Warehousing Clerk: Overseeing inventory levels, managing warehouse layouts, and ensuring efficient stock control.
  • Procurement Assistant: Supporting the purchasing department in sourcing materials, evaluating suppliers, and processing orders.
  • Public and Private Sector Roles: The TETA-accredited qualification is highly transferable, opening doors in government transport departments, retail distribution centers, and global freight forwarders.
  • Further Study: A strong foundation for pursuing a National Diploma or Degree in Supply Chain Management or Logistics while working.
