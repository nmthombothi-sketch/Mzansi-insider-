About This Learnership

FNB's Retail Banking Learnership is a premier 12-month programme that combines structured classroom training with hands-on, on-the-job experience in retail and business banking. Participants work toward an accredited Further Education and Training Certificate (FETC) in Banking at NQF Level 5.

As one of South Africa's "Big Four" banks, FNB is renowned for its innovation and employee development. This learnership is widely considered one of the strongest entry routes into the financial services sector for matriculants, with high performers frequently absorbed into permanent roles as tellers, banking consultants, or business banking specialists.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate. Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy is required.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 25 (though some intakes may extend to 35).
  • Employment Status: Must be currently unemployed and must not have completed a similar NQF 5 banking learnership previously.
  • Attributes: Exceptional verbal and written communication skills, a professional demeanor, and a genuine passion for helping customers.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any customer service, retail, or administrative experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in a career in banking and financial services

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you gain critical industry experience. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R5,000 per month. This is a training allowance intended to cover transport and basic living costs, not a full salary.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited Further Education and Training Certificate (FETC) in Banking at NQF Level 5, recognized across the entire financial services sector.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in FNB branches, learning about financial products, customer onboarding, and branch operations.
  • Career Pathway: Top performers are frequently fast-tracked for permanent positions as Tellers, Banking Consultants, or Business Banking Specialists.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the FNB learnership pool requires demonstrating a genuine passion for customer service and financial inclusion:

  • Highlight Customer Service: Mention any retail, hospitality, or volunteer experience where you helped people or resolved complaints.
  • Research FNB's Values: Understand FNB's mission of "creating a better world for clients, employees, and society." Mentioning this shows you align with their culture.
  • Practice Assessments: Brush up on basic numerical reasoning and reading comprehension tests, as these are standard in banking recruitment.
  • Be Professional: Ensure your email address is professional, your CV is error-free, and you respond promptly to any communication from the recruitment team.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 5 Banking learnership at a major bank opens several stable and rewarding career paths:

  • Banking Consultant / Teller: The most common progression, assisting clients with everyday banking needs, account openings, and product recommendations.
  • Business Banking Specialist: Supporting small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with their financial and operational needs.
  • Sales and Service Roles: Transitioning into target-driven roles focused on cross-selling financial products and building client relationships.
  • Further Study: The NQF 5 qualification provides credits that can be used to pursue a Higher Certificate or Diploma in Banking, Finance, or Business Management while working.
