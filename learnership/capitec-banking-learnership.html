About This Learnership

Capitec Bank's retail banking learnership is a highly sought-after 12-month programme designed to develop young South Africans in client service, bank operations, and financial advisory skills. Participants work toward an accredited NQF Level 4 Banking qualification while gaining hands-on experience in a fast-paced branch environment.

As one of South Africa's largest and fastest-growing banks, Capitec frequently absorbs high-performing learners into permanent roles, making this an exceptional launchpad for a long-term career in the financial services sector.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate. Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy is expected.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 30.
  • Employment Status: Must be currently unemployed and not enrolled in full-time tertiary studies.
  • Background Checks: Must have a clear criminal record and a clean credit history (mandatory for all banking roles).

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any customer service, retail, or administrative experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in a career in retail banking

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R6,000 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 4 Banking qualification, highly regarded across the financial services sector.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real Capitec branch, learning to assist clients, process transactions, and promote financial products.
  • Career Pathway: High performers are frequently considered for permanent Client Service Consultant or Personal Banker roles upon completion.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Capitec learnership pool requires demonstrating a genuine passion for customer service and financial inclusion:

  • Highlight Customer Service: Mention any retail, hospitality, or volunteer experience where you helped people or resolved complaints.
  • Research Capitec's Values: Understand Capitec's mission of "improving clients' financial lives." Mentioning this shows you align with their culture.
  • Practice Assessments: Brush up on basic numerical reasoning and reading comprehension tests, as these are standard in banking recruitment.
  • Be Professional: Ensure your email address is professional, your CV is error-free, and you respond promptly to any communication from the recruitment team.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 4 Banking learnership at a major retail bank opens several stable and rewarding career paths:

  • Client Service Consultant: The most common progression, assisting clients with everyday banking needs and product recommendations.
  • Teller or Branch Operations: Managing cash handling, account openings, and administrative branch functions.
  • Personal Banker: With strong performance, progressing to advise clients on loans, credit, and savings products.
  • Further Study: The NQF 4 qualification provides credits that can be used to pursue a Higher Certificate or Diploma in Banking or Financial Planning while working.
