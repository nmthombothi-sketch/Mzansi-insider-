About This Learnership

The Bookkeeping and Accounting Learnership, often facilitated through the FASSET SETA, is a highly practical 12-month programme designed to train learners in financial record-keeping, payroll processing, and financial documentation. It leads directly to an accredited NQF Level 4 qualification.

For young, numerate South Africans, this learnership serves as a powerful entry point into the finance industry. Every business, from small startups to large corporations, requires accurate bookkeeping, ensuring consistent demand for skilled junior finance professionals.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate. Mathematics or Accounting as a subject is highly advantageous.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Attributes: Highly numerate, detail-oriented, organized, and possessing strong ethical standards.
  • Technical Skills: Basic computer literacy, particularly familiarity with Microsoft Excel and data entry.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any administrative, retail, or numerical experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in a career in finance and bookkeeping

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R5,000 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited FASSET SETA NQF Level 4 Bookkeeping qualification, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real finance department, using accounting software (like Pastel, Sage, or Xero) to process invoices, manage ledgers, and reconcile accounts.
  • Career Foundation: A recognized stepping stone toward further accounting studies (like a Diploma in Accounting) or professional designations.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the bookkeeping learnership pool requires demonstrating reliability and a genuine interest in finance:

  • Highlight Numerical Experience: Mention any experience handling cash, managing budgets for a club, or doing administrative data entry, even informally.
  • Showcase Excel Skills: If you know how to use basic Excel formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, VLOOKUP), mention it prominently on your CV.
  • Emphasize Accuracy: Bookkeeping is all about precision. Use your motivational letter to give an example of a time your attention to detail prevented an error.
  • Be Professional: Ensure your email address is professional, your CV is error-free, and you respond promptly to any communication from the recruitment team.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 4 Bookkeeping learnership opens several stable and rewarding career paths:

  • Bookkeeper or Accounts Clerk: The immediate role upon completion, managing ledgers, invoices, and petty cash.
  • Payroll Administrator: Specializing in calculating wages, deductions, and ensuring compliance with SARS regulations.
  • Accounts Payable/Receivable Clerk: Managing money flowing in and out of a business, a critical function for any company.
  • Junior Accountant: With further part-time study (like a National Diploma or Degree), you can progress to full accounting roles.
  • Professional Designations: This learnership provides a foundation for pursuing designations like SAIPA (Trainee Accountant) or CIMA.
