About This Learnership

Impala Platinum's (Implats) Fitting and Engineering Learnership is a rigorous 24-month artisan development programme. It trains learners in mechanical fitting, maintenance, and engineering disciplines critical to the mining sector, combining structured theoretical learning with hands-on experience at Implats' world-class operations.

As one of the world's largest primary platinum producers, Implats provides unparalleled exposure to heavy industrial machinery and complex engineering systems. This learnership is a highly recognized and respected route toward becoming a qualified artisan, unlocking some of the strongest technical pay premiums in South Africa.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate with Mathematics and Physical Science (mandatory for technical streams).
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Physical Fitness: Must be medically fit and capable of performing physically demanding work in underground or surface mining environments.
  • Location Preference: Candidates residing in host communities near Implats' operations (e.g., Rustenburg, Limpopo) are frequently prioritised during selection.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate (must clearly show Mathematics and Physical Science)
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit, especially crucial for host community applicants)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any mechanical, technical, or manual work experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your passion for fitting, engineering, and the mining industry

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you master a highly specialized trade. Over the 24-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R6,000 per month to assist with transport, tools, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 3 technical qualification in Fitting or Engineering.
  • Practical Logbook Experience: Supervised, hands-on workplace experience that counts toward the mandatory hours required to write the national Artisan Trade Test.
  • Trade Test Preparation: Structured theoretical and practical training specifically designed to help you pass the artisan trade test and become a qualified fitter or mechanic.

Application Process

Step by step
1
Find the Opening: Monitor the Implats careers page for the Fitting & Engineering Learnership advert (typically early in the year).
2
Complete the Application: Fill out the online application form accurately, ensuring all technical subjects are highlighted.
3
Upload Documents: Attach your CV, certified ID, Matric certificate, and proof of residence in PDF format.
4
Assessments & Medicals: Shortlisted candidates will complete technical aptitude assessments and must pass a strict occupational medical examination.
5
Interview & Onboarding: Successful candidates attend a panel interview and, if selected, sign a learnership agreement to begin their 24-month training.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Implats learnership pool requires demonstrating a genuine aptitude for mechanical work and a strong safety mindset:

  • Highlight Mechanical Aptitude: Mention any hands-on experience with mechanics, welding, automotive repair, or DIY projects, even if it was informal.
  • Emphasize Safety: Mining is governed by strict safety laws (like the MHSA). Mention your understanding of the importance of workplace safety and following procedures.
  • Show Resilience: Mining environments can be physically and mentally demanding. Provide examples of your reliability, teamwork, and ability to work under pressure.
  • Proofread Everything: Ensure your CV and motivational letter are professional and free of errors, demonstrating your attention to detail.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing this learnership and passing your trade test opens the door to highly respected and well-compensated career paths in the resources sector:

  • Fitter or Mechanical Assistant: Supporting senior artisans with the maintenance and repair of heavy mining machinery.
  • Qualified Artisan: With additional experience and passing the trade test, becoming a fully qualified fitter, mechanic, or boilermaker.
  • Plant or Engineering Technician: Monitoring and optimizing the performance of processing plants and engineering systems.
  • Maintenance Specialist: With experience, moving into specialized roles in predictive maintenance, reliability engineering, or plant supervision.
  • Further Study: A strong foundation for pursuing a National Diploma in Mechanical or Mining Engineering.
