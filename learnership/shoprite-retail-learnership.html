About This Learnership

The Shoprite Checkers Retail Learnership is one of the most accessible and impactful entry-level programmes in South Africa. Facilitated through the W&R SETA (Wholesale and Retail Sector Education and Training Authority), this 12-month programme trains learners in wholesale and retail operations, leading to an accredited NQF Level 3 qualification.

Crucially, many intakes are open to applicants without a full Matric (Grade 10 or 11 is often accepted). This makes it a genuine, life-changing first step into the formal workforce for young South Africans, offering a stipend, real store experience, and strong prospects of permanent retail employment upon completion.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: Grade 10 or 11 certificate. (Note: Some specific intakes may prefer Matric, but many are explicitly designed for non-Matric candidates).
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 34.
  • Employment Status: Must be currently unemployed and not enrolled in any full-time tertiary studies.
  • Availability: Willingness to work flexible retail hours, including weekends, public holidays, and shift work.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your highest educational qualification (Grade 10, 11, or 12 certificate/statement of results)
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated CV highlighting any volunteer work, informal trading, or customer-facing experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining why you want to start your career in retail with Shoprite

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide a vital financial bridge while you gain formal workplace experience. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R3,100 per month to assist with transport and basic living costs during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited W&R SETA NQF Level 3 qualification in Wholesale and Retail Operations, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real Shoprite or Checkers store, learning about customer service, stock control, merchandising, and point-of-sale systems.
  • Career Pathway: A direct route into permanent retail employment, with many learners absorbed into the business as cashiers, supervisors, or management trainees.

Application Process

Step by step
1
Find the Opening: Visit the Shoprite Holdings careers page and search for the "Retail Learnership" listing.
2
Complete the Application: Fill out the online application form accurately, ensuring your contact details are correct.
3
Upload Documents: Attach your CV, certified ID, and educational documents in PDF format.
4
Assessments: Shortlisted candidates may be invited to complete a basic numeracy or literacy assessment.
5
Interview & Onboarding: Successful candidates will attend an interview at a local store and, if selected, sign a learnership agreement.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Shoprite learnership pool requires demonstrating reliability and a genuine willingness to learn:

  • Highlight Customer Service: Mention any informal experience helping people, such as volunteering, informal trading, or assisting in a community project.
  • Show Reliability: Retail requires punctuality and consistency. Provide examples of your dependability from school, sports, or previous informal work.
  • Express Willingness to Learn: Emphasize in your motivational letter that you are eager to gain formal qualifications and build a long-term career in the retail sector.
  • Proofread Everything: Ensure your CV and motivational letter are neat, professional, and free of errors, demonstrating your attention to detail.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 3 Retail learnership opens several stable and rewarding career paths in the massive retail sector:

  • Permanent Retail Roles: Direct absorption into positions such as Cashier, Merchandiser, or Floor Supervisor.
  • Store Management Trainee: High-performing learners are often fast-tracked into Shoprite's management development programmes.
  • Transferable Skills: Customer service, stock control, and teamwork experience that is highly valued across all retail and hospitality businesses.
  • Further Study: The NQF 3 qualification provides a foundation for pursuing higher retail or business management qualifications while working.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need Matric for the Shoprite learnership? +
Not always. Many Shoprite retail learnership intakes accept applicants without a full Matric (Grade 10 or 11 may be accepted), making it one of the most accessible programmes available.
How much does the Shoprite learnership pay? +
It pays a monthly stipend of around R3,100 over the 12-month programme while you complete an accredited NQF Level 3 retail qualification.
Can the Shoprite learnership lead to a job? +
Yes. Many learners are offered permanent roles as cashiers, merchandisers, or supervisors, and some progress onto store-management trainee pathways.
Who can apply for the Shoprite learnership? +
Unemployed South African citizens, typically aged 18–34, who are not studying full-time and are willing to work retail hours including weekends.
