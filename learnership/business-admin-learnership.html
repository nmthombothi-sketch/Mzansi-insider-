About This Learnership

The Business Administration Learnership, coordinated through the Services SETA, is a highly practical 12-month programme designed to prepare candidates for dynamic office and administrative careers. Training covers essential skills like office management, document control, business communication, and basic HR support, leading directly to an accredited NQF Level 4 qualification.

Because every organisation—from small startups to massive corporations—requires skilled administrative support, this learnership is one of the most versatile and accessible routes into formal employment for young South Africans.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Skills: Strong verbal and written communication skills, basic computer literacy (Microsoft Office), and a detail-oriented mindset.
  • History: Must not have completed a similar NQF Level 4 learnership previously.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any administrative, retail, or volunteer experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in building a career in business administration

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R4,500 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited Services SETA NQF Level 4 Business Administration qualification, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real office environment, learning to manage schedules, handle correspondence, and maintain filing systems.
  • Career Foundation: A highly portable qualification that serves as a stepping stone to further business studies (like a Diploma in Management) or specialized administrative roles.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the business administration learnership pool requires demonstrating reliability and professionalism:

  • Highlight Organizational Skills: Mention any experience managing schedules, handling data, or organizing events, even in a volunteer or school capacity.
  • Showcase Computer Literacy: If you are proficient in Microsoft Word, Excel, or PowerPoint, mention it prominently on your CV.
  • Proofread Everything: Attention to detail is the #1 skill for an administrator. Ensure your CV and motivational letter are completely free of spelling or grammatical errors.
  • Be Professional: Use a professional email address, dress appropriately for interviews, and respond promptly to any communication from the recruitment team.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 4 Business Administration learnership opens several stable and rewarding career paths across every industry:

  • Office Administrator / Administrative Assistant: The immediate role upon completion, managing daily office operations and supporting management.
  • Data Capturer or Document Controller: Specializing in accurate data entry and maintaining organized digital or physical filing systems.
  • Receptionist or Office Coordinator: Serving as the first point of contact for clients and managing front-desk operations.
  • HR or Operations Assistant: Supporting human resources or operations teams with onboarding, scheduling, and compliance tasks.
  • Further Study: A strong foundation for pursuing a Higher Certificate or Diploma in Business Management, Office Administration, or Human Resources.
