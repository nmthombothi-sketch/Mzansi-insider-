About This Learnership

Eskom's Electrical Engineering Learnership is one of the most prestigious and sought-after technical development programmes in South Africa. This rigorous 24-month programme trains learners in electrical disciplines across power generation, transmission, and distribution systems, combining structured theoretical learning with hands-on experience at Eskom's world-class training facilities and power stations.

As the national power utility, Eskom provides unparalleled exposure to large-scale electrical infrastructure, making this learnership an exceptional launchpad for a career in the energy sector. Graduates are highly regarded across the engineering industry.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate with Mathematics and Physical Science (mandatory for engineering streams).
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Technical Aptitude: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a genuine interest in electrical and power engineering.
  • Physical Requirements: Willingness to work at power stations, substations, and technical sites, which may involve shift work and physically demanding environments.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate (must show Mathematics and Physical Science)
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any technical, mechanical, or engineering-related experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your passion for electrical engineering and the energy sector

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you master highly specialized technical skills. Over the 24-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R6,500 per month — one of the highest stipends offered by any South African learnership.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 4 qualification in Electrical Engineering, recognized nationwide.
  • World-Class Training: Hands-on experience at Eskom's power stations, substations, and technical training centers, working with real industrial equipment.
  • Career Pathway: A direct route into the energy sector, with opportunities to write trade tests and progress to senior technical roles.

Application Process

Step by step
1
Find the Opening: Monitor the Eskom careers portal for the Electrical Engineering Learnership announcement (typically early in the year).

Create a Profile: Register on the Eskom recruitment portal and complete your candidate profile accurately.
3
Upload Documents: Attach your CV, certified ID, Matric certificate (showing Maths and Science), and motivational letter.

Complete Assessments: Shortlisted candidates are usually invited to complete technical aptitude, numerical reasoning, and psychometric assessments.
5
Interview & Onboarding: Successful candidates attend a technical panel interview and, if selected, sign a learnership agreement to begin training.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Eskom learnership pool requires demonstrating exceptional technical aptitude and a genuine passion for the energy sector:

  • Highlight Academic Performance: Emphasize your marks in Mathematics and Physical Science. Strong performance in these subjects is critical for selection.
  • Show Technical Interest: Mention any hands-on experience with electronics, robotics, DIY electrical projects, or engineering-related hobbies.
  • Research Eskom's Operations: Demonstrate knowledge of Eskom's role in power generation, transmission, and distribution. Mentioning current energy challenges shows genuine interest.
  • Prepare for Assessments: Practice numerical reasoning, logical thinking, and basic electrical theory questions. These assessments are competitive.
  • Apply Early: Eskom learnerships receive thousands of applications. Submitting a complete, polished application as early as possible demonstrates organization and seriousness.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 4 Electrical Engineering learnership at Eskom opens several prestigious and well-compensated career paths:

  • Electrical Technician: Working in power stations or substations, maintaining and troubleshooting electrical systems.
  • Plant Operator: Operating and monitoring power generation equipment, ensuring safe and efficient plant operations.
  • Engineering Technician: Supporting senior engineers with technical projects, maintenance planning, and system upgrades.
  • Artisan (with Trade Test): With additional training and passing the trade test, becoming a qualified electrician or millwright.
  • Further Study: A strong foundation for pursuing a National Diploma in Electrical Engineering or a BEng Tech degree while working.
