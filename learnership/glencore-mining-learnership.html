About This Learnership

Glencore's Engineering and Mining Learnership is a rigorous 24-month programme designed to develop the next generation of technical talent for the resources sector. It provides hands-on workplace training in mining operations, mechanical maintenance, and electrical systems, combining structured theoretical learning with practical experience at Glencore's operational sites.

As one of the world's largest diversified natural resource companies, Glencore offers unparalleled exposure to large-scale mining operations. Furthermore, the mining sector consistently offers some of the strongest technical pay premiums in South Africa, making this an exceptional launchpad for a lucrative and stable career.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate with Mathematics and Physical Science (mandatory for technical streams).
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Physical Fitness: Must be medically fit and capable of performing physically demanding work in underground or open-pit mining environments.
  • Location Preference: Candidates residing in host communities near Glencore's operations (e.g., Mpumalanga, North West) are often prioritised.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate (must show Mathematics and Physical Science)
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit, especially if applying as a host community candidate)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any technical, mechanical, or manual work experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your passion for the mining industry and technical work

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you master highly specialized technical skills. Over the 24-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R6,000 per month to assist with transport, tools, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 3 technical qualification in Engineering or Mining Operations.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on, supervised workplace experience in real mining environments, working with heavy machinery and industrial systems.
  • Career Pathway: A direct route into the mining sector, with opportunities to write trade tests and progress to senior technical or artisan roles.

Application Process

Step by step
1
Find the Opening: Monitor the Glencore careers page for the Engineering & Mining Learnership advert (typically early in the year).
2
Complete the Application: Fill out the online application form accurately, ensuring all technical subjects are highlighted.
3
Upload Documents: Attach your CV, certified ID, Matric certificate, and proof of residence in PDF format.
4
Assessments & Medicals: Shortlisted candidates will complete technical aptitude assessments and must pass a strict occupational medical examination.
5
Interview & Onboarding: Successful candidates attend a panel interview and, if selected, sign a learnership agreement to begin training.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Glencore learnership pool requires demonstrating a genuine aptitude for technical work and a strong safety mindset:

  • Highlight Technical Experience: Mention any hands-on experience with mechanics, welding, electronics, or DIY projects, even if it was informal.
  • Emphasize Safety: Mining is governed by strict safety laws (like the MHSA). Mention your understanding of the importance of workplace safety and following procedures.
  • Show Resilience: Mining environments can be physically and mentally demanding. Provide examples of your reliability, teamwork, and ability to work under pressure.
  • Proofread Everything: Ensure your CV and motivational letter are professional and free of errors, demonstrating your attention to detail.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing this learnership opens the door to highly respected and well-compensated career paths in the resources sector:

  • Mining or Engineering Assistant: Supporting senior technicians and engineers with maintenance and operational tasks.
  • Artisan (with Trade Test): Progressing to become a qualified fitter, electrician, or boilermaker, commanding high industry salaries.
  • Process or Plant Operator: Operating and monitoring heavy machinery and processing plants to ensure efficient production.
  • Technical Specialist: With experience, moving into specialized roles in ventilation, rock engineering, or plant maintenance.
  • Further Study: A strong foundation for pursuing a National Diploma in Mining Engineering or related technical fields.
