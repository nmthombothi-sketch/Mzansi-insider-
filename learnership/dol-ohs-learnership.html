About This Learnership

The Department of Employment and Labour's Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Learnership is a vital 12-month programme designed to equip young South Africans with the skills to maintain safe, compliant, and hazard-free workplaces. Participants work toward an accredited NQF Level 4 qualification in Occupational Health and Safety.

Because OHS legislation mandates that all businesses, from construction sites to corporate offices, must have designated safety representatives, this qualification is incredibly versatile. It offers a stable, future-proof career path with demand across every major industry in South Africa.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Attributes: Highly observant, detail-oriented, responsible, and possessing strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Interest: A genuine interest in workplace safety, risk management, and regulatory compliance.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any leadership, organizational, or safety-related experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in occupational health and safety

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and critical, life-saving education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R4,000 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 4 Occupational Health and Safety qualification, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training conducting risk assessments, facilitating safety meetings, and ensuring compliance with the OHS Act.
  • Career Foundation: A highly portable qualification that allows you to work in construction, mining, manufacturing, corporate offices, or government.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the OHS learnership pool requires demonstrating responsibility and a proactive mindset:

  • Highlight Attention to Detail: Give examples from school, sports, or previous jobs where your careful observation prevented a mistake or improved a process.
  • Show Leadership: OHS roles require enforcing rules and guiding others. Mention any experience as a prefect, team captain, or community leader.
  • Research the OHS Act: Briefly mentioning your awareness of the Occupational Health and Safety Act (Act 85 of 1993) in your motivational letter shows genuine initiative.
  • Proofread Everything: Safety is about precision. Ensure your CV and motivational letter are completely free of spelling or grammatical errors.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 4 OHS learnership opens several stable and rewarding career paths across multiple industries:

  • Health and Safety Officer / SHE Representative: The immediate role upon completion, monitoring workplace conditions and ensuring compliance with safety regulations.
  • Compliance and Risk-Assessment Assistant: Helping organizations identify potential hazards and develop mitigation strategies.
  • Industry-Specific Safety Roles: Opportunities in high-demand sectors like construction, mining, manufacturing, and logistics.
  • Further Study: A strong foundation for pursuing advanced, highly respected OHS qualifications like SAMTRAC, NEBOSH, or a National Diploma in Safety Management.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the OHS learnership pay? +
It pays a monthly stipend of around R4,000 over the 12-month programme while you complete an accredited NQF Level 4 occupational health and safety qualification.
Is occupational health and safety a good career? +
Yes. OHS skills are required in every industry — construction, manufacturing, mining, retail — which makes the qualification highly versatile and the roles relatively secure.
Who can apply for the OHS learnership? +
South African citizens (typically 18–35) with a Grade 12 / Matric certificate, an interest in health, safety, and compliance, and strong attention to detail.
What can the OHS learnership lead to? +
Roles such as Health and Safety Officer, SHE Representative, or Compliance Assistant, and it provides a strong foundation for further OHS qualifications like SAMTRAC or NEBOSH.
