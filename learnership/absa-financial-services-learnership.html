About This Learnership

Absa's Financial Services Learnership is a premier 12-month programme designed to develop young South Africans in banking and financial-services operations. Participants work toward an accredited NQF Level 5 qualification while gaining invaluable hands-on workplace experience.

The programme blends structured classroom learning with practical on-the-job training. High performers are frequently absorbed into permanent consultant, sales, and operations roles, making this a highly structured and paid entry point into the financial-services industry for ambitious matriculants.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate with Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 28.
  • Skills: Strong numeracy, excellent verbal and written communication skills, and basic computer literacy.
  • History: Must not have completed a similar NQF Level 5 learnership previously.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any volunteer, retail, or informal work experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in the banking and financial services sector

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R4,700 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 5 Financial Services qualification, highly regarded across the banking sector.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real banking environment, dealing with customer queries, financial products, and operational processes.
  • Mentorship: Guidance from experienced Absa professionals to help you navigate your career path.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Absa learnership pool requires demonstrating both potential and professionalism:

  • Tailor Your CV: Highlight any customer service, retail, or cash-handling experience, even if it was informal or volunteer work.
  • Research Absa: Understand Absa's "Africa Pulse" strategy and their focus on financial inclusion. Mentioning this shows genuine interest.
  • Practice Assessments: Brush up on basic numeracy and verbal reasoning tests, as these are standard in banking recruitment.
  • Be Professional: Ensure your email address is professional, your CV is error-free, and you respond promptly to any communication from the recruitment team.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 5 Financial Services learnership at a major bank is a powerful career catalyst:

  • Permanent Banking Roles: High performers are frequently absorbed into permanent Banking Consultant, Sales Agent, or Client Service roles.
  • Operations & Support: Opportunities in back-office operations, compliance, or fraud detection.
  • Further Education: The NQF 5 qualification provides credits that can be used to pursue higher qualifications (like a Diploma or Degree in Banking or Finance) while working.
  • Industry Mobility: The skills and accreditation gained are highly transferable across the entire South African financial services sector.
