About This Learnership

The Long-Term Insurance Learnership, facilitated through INSETA (Insurance Sector Education and Training Authority), is a highly practical 12-month programme. It trains learners in insurance products, claims processing, risk assessment, and customer service, leading directly to an accredited NQF Level 4 qualification.

The insurance industry is a stable, sizeable employer in South Africa. This learnership provides a direct entry point into the sector, offering clear progression paths for matriculants who demonstrate strong communication skills and a willingness to learn about financial protection and risk management.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate. Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy is acceptable.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Skills: Excellent verbal and written communication skills, basic computer literacy, and a professional demeanor.
  • Attributes: A genuine interest in the financial services industry, with strong attention to detail and a customer-first mindset.

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or official statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any customer service, retail, or administrative experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter explaining your interest in a career in insurance and financial services

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to support you financially while you gain critical industry experience. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R5,000 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses during the training period.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited INSETA NQF Level 4 qualification in Long-Term Insurance, recognized nationwide.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real insurance environment, learning about policy administration, claims handling, and client relations.
  • Career Foundation: A strong stepping stone toward becoming a licensed financial advisor, broker, or underwriting specialist.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the insurance learnership pool requires demonstrating professionalism and a genuine interest in helping people:

  • Highlight Customer Service: Mention any retail, hospitality, or volunteer experience where you helped people, resolved complaints, or provided excellent service.
  • Show Attention to Detail: Insurance involves paperwork and compliance. Give examples of your accuracy and organizational skills.
  • Research the Industry: Briefly mention your understanding of what insurance does (protecting people and businesses from risk) in your motivational letter.
  • Proofread Everything: Ensure your CV and motivational letter are professional and completely free of spelling or grammatical errors.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing an NQF 4 Insurance learnership opens several stable and rewarding career paths in the financial services sector:

  • Insurance Claims Administrator: Processing and managing insurance claims efficiently and fairly.
  • Customer Service Consultant: Assisting policyholders with queries, updates, and product information.
  • Underwriting Assistant: Supporting underwriters in assessing risk and determining policy terms.
  • Broker Support / Sales: With experience, moving into client-facing roles to advise on and sell insurance products.
  • Pathway to Financial Advisory: A strong foundation for obtaining the necessary RE5 and RE1 regulatory exams to become a licensed Financial Services Provider (FSP).
