About This Learnership

The Contact Centre and Customer Service Learnership, often facilitated through the Services SETA, prepares learners for dynamic roles in South Africa's booming Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) and call-centre industry. It builds critical communication, problem-solving, and client-relationship skills.

South Africa has become a top global destination for outsourcing due to its neutral accent, strong infrastructure, and favorable time zones. This learnership provides a paid entry point into a sector that hires at scale and offers rapid career progression for dedicated individuals.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Education: A certified Grade 12 (Matric) certificate is mandatory.
  • Age: Typically targeted at unemployed youth between the ages of 18 and 35.
  • Communication: Clear, articulate spoken English and a professional, empathetic phone manner.
  • Technical Skills: Basic computer literacy (typing, navigating software, using email).

Required Documents Checklist

Before applying, ensure you have clear, certified copies (not older than 3 months) of the following documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your Grade 12 / Matric certificate or statement of results
  • ✅ Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill or affidavit)
  • ✅ An updated, professional CV highlighting any volunteer or informal work experience
  • ✅ A brief motivational letter or email explaining why you want to work in customer service

Stipend & What You Get

This learnership is designed to provide both income and education simultaneously. Over the 12-month period, you will receive:

  • Monthly Stipend: Approximately R5,000 per month to assist with transport, data, and living expenses.
  • Formal Qualification: An accredited NQF Level 3 qualification in Contact Centre Operations or Customer Service.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on training in a real call-centre environment, handling inbound or outbound queries.
  • Soft Skills Development: Training in conflict resolution, active listening, and professional communication.

Career Opportunities After Completion

Completing a contact centre learnership is not a dead-end; it is a springboard. The BPO sector rewards performance and offers clear progression paths:

  • Customer Service / Call Centre Agent: The immediate role upon completion, handling queries for local or international brands.
  • Technical or Sales Support: Specializing in higher-value accounts that pay premium commissions.
  • Team Leader / Supervisor: Managing a floor of agents, typically achievable within 1–2 years of strong performance.
  • Quality Assurance (QA) or Trainer: Moving off the phones to monitor calls, coach new agents, and develop training materials.
  • International BPO Roles: Leveraging experience to work for top-tier global outsourcing firms with higher salary bands.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the contact centre learnership pay? +
It pays a monthly stipend of around R5,000 over a 12-month period, plus you earn a recognized NQF Level 3 customer-service qualification.
Is the call-centre sector hiring in South Africa? +
Yes. South Africa is a rapidly growing global outsourcing (BPO) hub, and the contact-centre sector hires at scale, including for lucrative international accounts.
What do you need for a contact centre learnership? +
A Grade 12 (Matric) certificate, clear spoken English, a good phone manner, basic computer literacy, and a customer-focused attitude.
Can a contact centre job lead anywhere? +
Yes. Agents can progress to team leader, quality assurance, training, and international-account management roles with experience and further upskilling.
