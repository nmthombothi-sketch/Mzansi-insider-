Reselling & Thrift Flipping in South Africa

One of the lowest-effort, lowest-risk hustles: start by selling things you already own, then flip underpriced second-hand finds for profit.

Overview

Reselling means buying underpriced items — thrifted clothing, vintage furniture, collectibles, or sale-rack bargains — and selling them at a markup. You can start with zero risk by first selling items you no longer use, learning the platforms and process before spending anything on new stock.

Marketplaces like Facebook Marketplace, Yaga, and Gumtree make listing easy, and the model scales naturally as you learn what sells best in your local area or niche.

Income Breakdown at a Glance

Reselling Niche Estimated Monthly Earnings (After Costs)
Decluttering Personal Items R500 – R2 000 (Pure profit, no inventory cost)
Thrifted Clothing & Sneakers R2 000 – R6 000 (High demand on Yaga & Marketplace)
Vintage Furniture & Collectibles R4 000 – R10 000+ (Higher ticket, lower volume)

How to Start

Step by step
1
Sell items you already own on Facebook Marketplace or Yaga to learn the ropes and build initial capital.
2
Study what sells well and at what price in your specific area or chosen online niche.
3
Source underpriced stock from thrift stores, clearance sales, garage sales, and online bargain groups.
4
Photograph and list items clearly in good lighting, with honest, detailed descriptions to build buyer trust.
5
Reinvest your profits into more stock and gradually build toward a specific, profitable niche.

Pros

  • ✅ Near-zero startup risk
  • ✅ Start immediately with what you own
  • ✅ Flexible and low-pressure schedule
  • ✅ Scales naturally with your effort and knowledge

Cons

  • ❌ Income depends heavily on sourcing skill
  • ❌ Time spent hunting for stock can be unpredictable
  • ❌ Physical storage can become an issue as you scale
  • ❌ Some items may sit unsold for weeks or months

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can you earn reselling in South Africa?
Realistic earnings run R1 000–R10 000 a month depending on time invested and sourcing skill. The key is buying underpriced stock and selling at a markup.
How do you start reselling with no money?
Start by selling items you already own on Facebook Marketplace or Yaga. This earns pure profit and teaches you the platforms before you spend anything on stock.
Where can you sell second-hand items in South Africa?
Facebook Marketplace, Yaga, and Gumtree are the most popular platforms, each making it easy to list and reach local buyers.
What items are best for reselling?
Designer clothing, vintage furniture, and collectibles tend to flip well, but the real skill is buying anything underpriced and selling it at a fair markup.
