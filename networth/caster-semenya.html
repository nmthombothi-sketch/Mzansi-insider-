Source of Wealth

Caster Semenya's fortune of around $18 million in 2026 reflects years of substantial prize money, performance bonuses, global endorsements, and speaking appearances built on one of the most dominant middle-distance records in athletics history.

At her peak, she was almost unbeatable over 800m — winning two Olympic golds and three world titles, alongside a remarkable 30-race winning streak over the distance. This unprecedented dominance made her a highly attractive and recognizable figure for major international sponsors.

Her public profile has been shaped as much by her long, principled legal battle over World Athletics' testosterone regulations as by her medals. In July 2025, she won a significant appeal on the grounds that she had been denied a fair trial, further cementing her status as a global advocate for human rights in sports.

How many Olympic golds has Caster Semenya won?
She won two Olympic gold medals in the women's 800m (2012 and 2016), along with three World Championship titles in the event.
