Source of Wealth

Wendy Appelbaum's fortune, estimated at around $300 million in 2026, stems from the Liberty Life empire built by her father, the legendary insurance magnate Donald Gordon, combined with her own astute personal investing.

She was deeply involved in building Liberty International and has held significant interests across the financial services sector. Beyond finance, she is the proud owner of the acclaimed De Morgenzon wine estate in Stellenbosch, a passion project that has won numerous international awards.

She is also one of the country's most prominent philanthropists and an outspoken advocate for ethics in business, corporate governance, and women's economic empowerment, using her wealth as a platform for meaningful change.
