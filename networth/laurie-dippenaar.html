Source of Wealth

Laurie Dippenaar's fortune of roughly $1.2 billion in 2026 comes from co-founding the businesses that became FirstRand — one of South Africa's biggest and most innovative financial-services groups, spanning FNB, Rand Merchant Bank (RMB), and WesBank.

His wealth is deeply rooted in long-held equity stakes through vehicles such as RMB Holdings and RMI, built up over a career that helped shape modern South African banking and insurance. Unlike many who cash out early, Dippenaar's strategy has been one of patient, decades-long compounding.

He is widely regarded as one of the principal architects of a distinctly entrepreneurial, owner-operator banking culture in South Africa, having started from a small merchant-banking base in the late 1970s and scaling it into a continental powerhouse.
