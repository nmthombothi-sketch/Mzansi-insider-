Jannie Mouton Net Worth

Fired from his own firm at 50, Jannie Mouton turned humiliation into a fortune — building PSG Group into an investment powerhouse and earning the nickname the 'Boere Buffett'.

Source of Wealth

Jannie Mouton's fortune of roughly $1.3 billion in 2026 stems from PSG Group, the investment holding company he founded in 1995 after being unceremoniously ousted from the stockbroking firm he had co-founded.

His most legendary bet was backing Capitec Bank in its infancy. PSG's strategic stakes across Capitec, Curro (private education), Zeder (agriculture), and PSG Konsult (financial services) compounded into one of post-apartheid South Africa's greatest investment success stories.

He holds a significant stake (roughly 5%) in Capitec through his family trust, and that exposure alone underpins much of his billionaire status as the bank's shares have consistently climbed over the decades.
