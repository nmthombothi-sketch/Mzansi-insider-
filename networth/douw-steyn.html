Source of Wealth

Douw Steyn's fortune of around $1.5 billion in 2026 is built on the BGL Group, the highly successful UK insurance business behind comparethemarket.com and other direct-insurance brands, alongside major property developments.

He pioneered direct and comparison insurance models that proved hugely profitable in the UK market. Rather than simply extracting profits, he strategically reinvested his wealth into landmark South African real estate, including the iconic Saxon Hotel and the vast, premium Steyn City development north of Johannesburg.

As a privately held fortune spanning international insurance and domestic property, the exact figure is an estimate, but his status as one of South Africa's most successful self-made entrepreneurs remains undisputed.
