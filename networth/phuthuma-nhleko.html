Source of Wealth

Phuthuma Nhleko's fortune, estimated around $400 million in 2026, was built through his transformative leadership of MTN Group — Africa's largest mobile operator — and a robust portfolio of investments and advisory roles assembled over his decades-long career.

He served as MTN's CEO during a defining era of explosive expansion across Africa and the Middle East, and later returned as chairman to steer the company through a critical turnaround period, building both immense wealth and continental influence in the process.

Beyond MTN, his private investment vehicle, Phembani Group, and his strategic board roles across major South African companies have diversified his wealth well beyond a single executive salary or equity stake. As a largely private fortune, the figure is an approximation.
