Source of Wealth

Gary Player's net worth of around $250 million in 2026 makes him one of golf's wealthiest figures, built far more on visionary business ventures than on the relatively modest prize money of his playing era.

A career Grand Slam winner with nine major championships, 'The Black Knight' brilliantly leveraged his global fame into Gary Player Design. This prolific firm has designed or renovated hundreds of golf courses across six continents. He also expanded into apparel, branding, and bloodstock interests.

Player is the ultimate model of converting sporting fame into an enduring, multi-generational enterprise. As with all celebrity figures, these net worth estimates vary by source.

Career History

1935
