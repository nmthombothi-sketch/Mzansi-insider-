Source of Wealth

Ivan Glasenberg's roughly $11.2 billion fortune comes almost entirely from his massive shareholding in Glencore, the commodities trading and mining giant he led as CEO from 2002 to 2021.

His wealth is intrinsically tied to the global trade in metals, oil, coal, and agricultural products. Glencore's blockbuster 2011 IPO crystallised enormous paper wealth for its trader-owners, with Glasenberg foremost among them as the largest individual shareholder.

Even after stepping down as CEO, his long-term equity stake keeps him among the wealthiest South African-born individuals globally, though Forbes typically excludes him from its South Africa resident list due to his overseas residency.
