Source of Wealth

Patrice Motsepe's fortune of around $4.3 billion in 2026 spans diversified mining, gold, digital banking, and renewable energy. He made his initial fortune in the mining sector and has steadily broadened his portfolio into financial services and technology.

He founded African Rainbow Minerals (ARM) after strategically buying and turning around marginal gold-mine shafts in the 1990s — taking on assets that major corporations viewed as played out and making them highly profitable through efficient management.

Through African Rainbow Capital, he has built significant exposure to financial services, including digital banking, and renewable energy, successfully diversifying his wealth well beyond the volatile mining cycle that made him a billionaire.

Career History

The road to the fortune
1962
Born in Soweto; learns fundamental business principles working in his father's township shop.
1980s
Qualifies as a lawyer; becomes the first Black partner at a major South African law firm.
1994
Enters the mining sector as contract work opens up; starts buying marginal, abandoned gold shafts.
1997
Founds African Rainbow Minerals, turning around assets that major mining houses had abandoned.
2000s
Becomes South Africa's first Black billionaire; begins diversifying into capital markets and finance.
2020–2026
Leads African football administration (CAF); aggressively expands into digital banking and renewable energy.
