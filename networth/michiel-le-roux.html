Source of Wealth

Michiel le Roux's fortune of roughly $1.9 billion in 2026 rests almost entirely on his founding stake of around 11% in Capitec Bank, which he helped start in 2001 and which is now South Africa's largest retail bank by customer numbers.

Capitec's relentless share-price growth has been the primary driver of his wealth. The stock has repeatedly surged over the years, with major rallies adding hundreds of millions of dollars to his personal stake in a matter of weeks, compounding his initial investment exponentially.

Beyond the balance sheet, le Roux is also notable for his civic engagement. He made the largest individual political donation in South African history, contributing R58 million to the Democratic Alliance over a twelve-month period, underscoring his influence beyond the financial sector.

What aspiring builders can take away
