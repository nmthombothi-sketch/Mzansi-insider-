Source of Wealth

Desmond Sacco's fortune of roughly $2.5 billion in 2026 comes from the Assore Group, the mining company founded by his father that he expertly built into a major global producer of manganese, iron ore, and chrome.

His wealth is closely tied to Assore's substantial stake in Assmang, a premier ferrous-metals producer. These commodities are essential inputs for steel, and their prices have historically benefited from sustained global industrial demand, particularly from emerging markets.

As a private mining fortune, the precise figure is harder to pin down than for listed-share billionaires, but Sacco is consistently ranked among South Africa's wealthiest individuals, having successfully stewarded and multiplied a family legacy.
