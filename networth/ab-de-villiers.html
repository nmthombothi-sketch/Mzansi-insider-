Source of Wealth

AB de Villiers' fortune of around $20 million (approximately R265 million) in 2026 was built on a stellar 15-year international career, a hugely lucrative Indian Premier League (IPL) run, and a string of premium global endorsements.

The IPL was the single biggest driver of his wealth. Across his career, primarily with the Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB), he earned over R223 million in the league alone. This monumental figure makes him the highest-paid South African player in IPL history, showcasing how global franchise cricket can exponentially multiply an athlete's earnings beyond national contracts.

Since retiring from international cricket in 2018, he has continued to generate significant income through franchise T20 leagues, high-profile commentary roles, and enduring endorsements with premium brands like Puma and Montblanc.
