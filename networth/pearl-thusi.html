Pearl Thusi's net worth of around $2.5 million in 2026 comes from a highly diversified entertainment portfolio spanning acting, television presenting, and her own entrepreneurial ventures in the beauty and haircare industry.

Beyond screen work, she has successfully leveraged her massive social media influence to build entrepreneurial ventures, notably in haircare. As with most celebrities, her exact net worth is an estimate that varies by source.
