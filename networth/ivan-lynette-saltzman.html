Source of Wealth

Ivan and Lynette Saltzman's combined fortune of roughly $1 billion in 2026 comes almost entirely from Dis-Chem Pharmacies, the retail pharmacy and health chain they founded and meticulously built over four decades.

Dis-Chem's landmark listing on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE) in 2016 crystallised the family's wealth, transforming a privately held business into a publicly valued empire. The couple retains a significant, influential shareholding in the business they started from a single store in Durban.

The chain's innovative model — seamlessly combining traditional pharmacy services with beauty, healthy foods, and supplements at scale — turned a modest start-up into a household name with hundreds of outlets across southern Africa.

Lessons Learned

What aspiring builders can take away
  • Start Small, Think Big: A single store and modest capital can grow into a national powerhouse with relentless focus and customer-centric innovation.
  • Combine Convenience: Pairing traditional pharmacy services with beauty, food, and supplements widened their market appeal and increased basket size.
