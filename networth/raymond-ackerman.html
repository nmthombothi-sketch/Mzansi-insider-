Source of Wealth

The Ackerman family fortune, estimated at around $300 million in 2026, is built almost entirely on Pick n Pay, the supermarket group with hundreds of stores across South Africa and neighboring countries.

Raymond Ackerman, who passed away in 2023, founded the modern Pick n Pay after being fired from the Greatermans/Checkers group. He bought four small stores and applied his revolutionary philosophy of "consumer sovereignty," fundamentally changing the South African retail landscape.

Today, the family retains a significant controlling stake in the JSE-listed group. Raymond's widow, Wendy, and the wider Ackerman family remain central to its ownership and strategic direction, ensuring the legacy endures.
