Source of Wealth

The Fergusons' combined net worth of around $12 million reflects their dual success as beloved actors and, more importantly, as the visionary owners of Ferguson Films. The production company is behind some of South Africa's most iconic and long-running dramas, including The Queen, Rockville, and iGazi.

Their wealth trajectory is a masterclass in media entrepreneurship: they moved beyond performing into ownership. By producing and selling shows directly to major broadcasters, they captured far more backend value and intellectual property rights than acting fees alone could provide.

Although Shona Ferguson tragically passed away in 2021, the production business and the powerful brand they built together continue to thrive under Connie Ferguson's steadfast leadership. As private-company owners, their exact valuations are estimates.
