Source of Wealth

Wendy Ackerman's fortune, estimated at around $300 million in 2026, comes almost entirely from the Pick n Pay retail empire she co-founded with her late husband, Raymond Ackerman, in 1967.

She has served as a non-executive director of the group for many years, and the Ackerman family continues to own a substantial, controlling stake in the JSE-listed company, ensuring their legacy endures.

Widely regarded as one of South Africa's most influential and pioneering businesswomen, she has devoted much of her later life to philanthropy, education, and community upliftment, turning generational wealth into lasting social impact.
