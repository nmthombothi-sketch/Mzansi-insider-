Nathan Kirsh Net Worth

A 94-year-old who grew up in Potchefstroom, lost most of his early fortune, rebuilt from a single Brooklyn grocery store, and in 2026 became Africa's second-richest person — almost entirely out of the public spotlight.

Source of Wealth

Nathan Kirsh's fortune climbed toward an estimated $20 billion in 2026, vaulting him to Africa's second-richest person behind Aliko Dangote. The primary catalyst was Sysco's agreement in March 2026 to acquire his Jetro Restaurant Depot business in a deal valued at approximately $29.1 billion (including debt) — one of the largest food-distribution transactions in history, adding roughly $10 billion to his personal fortune.

His best-known asset, Jetro Restaurant Depot, became a multibillion-dollar wholesaler by supplying independent restaurants, caterers, and small businesses that larger, corporate distributors consistently overlooked. It is a quiet, low-margin model executed at vast, unprecedented scale.

Remarkably, Kirsh survived a devastating early business collapse in the 1980s that wiped out much of his initial wealth. He rebuilt from a single cash-and-carry store in Brooklyn, New York, eventually assembling a global portfolio across food distribution, commercial property, and finance in near-total obscurity.

What aspiring builders can take away
