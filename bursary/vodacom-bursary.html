About This Bursary

The Vodacom External Bursary is designed to fund full-time undergraduate students in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) fields who may lack the financial resources to pursue higher education. Beyond full financial coverage, the programme provides a laptop and cellphone, an orientation workshop, structured vacation work, and work-readiness training for final-year students to ensure a smooth transition into the corporate world.

Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen by birth.
  • Enrollment: Must be registered or planning to register for full-time undergraduate study at an accredited South African tertiary institution.
  • Academic Performance (Matric): Grade 12 exemption with a minimum of 70%+ in all subjects.
  • Academic Performance (Tertiary): Minimum 65% average if already studying at a tertiary level.
  • Field of Study: Must be pursuing a degree in a recognized STEM field (e.g., Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, Actuarial Science).

What It Covers

  • Full Tuition & Accommodation: Complete coverage of university registration, tuition fees, and accredited residence costs.
  • Living Allowance: Provision for meals and a dedicated book allowance.
  • Technology: A brand-new laptop and cellphone to ensure students have the tools needed for modern learning.
  • Career Development: Structured vacation work programmes and comprehensive work-readiness training for final-year students.
