About This Bursary

The Mercedes-Benz South Africa Bursary is a prestigious corporate initiative designed to develop the next generation of technical and business leaders for the automotive industry. It funds high-potential students in engineering, IT, and finance, offering full financial coverage alongside unparalleled exposure to premium vehicle manufacturing.

Centered primarily around its world-class plant in East London, Eastern Cape, this bursary provides recipients with paid vacation work, mentorship from industry experts, and a direct pathway to graduate employment within one of the most respected automotive brands globally.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Exceptional Matric results, particularly in Mathematics and Physical Science. Current university students must maintain a strong academic average.
  • Field of Study: Studying or intending to study Mechanical, Mechatronics, or Industrial Engineering, Information Technology, or Finance at a recognized South African university.
  • Industry Interest: A genuine passion for the automotive industry, manufacturing processes, and technological innovation.
  • Leadership Potential: Demonstrated leadership skills, teamwork, and extracurricular involvement.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have the following certified documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration
  • ✅ A detailed CV highlighting academic achievements, projects, and leadership roles
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for the automotive industry and why you want to join Mercedes-Benz SA
  • ✅ Proof of household income (if applying based on financial need)

What It Covers

The Mercedes-Benz SA Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to remove financial barriers and provide holistic support for your academic and professional development:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks and a brand-new laptop.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Practical Experience: Paid vacation work at the Mercedes-Benz SA plant, providing real-world exposure to premium automotive manufacturing.
  • Career Pathway: Mentorship from industry professionals and strong potential for a graduate role upon successful completion of your studies.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Mercedes-Benz bursary pool requires demonstrating both academic brilliance and a genuine passion for the industry:

  • Show Industry Knowledge: Research Mercedes-Benz SA's operations, particularly the East London plant's role in global exports and its focus on sustainability and innovation. Mention this in your motivational letter.
  • Highlight Practical Projects: If you've participated in robotics clubs, coding competitions, or engineering projects, highlight them. Practical application of your studies is highly valued.
  • Demonstrate Problem-Solving: Use your CV and motivational letter to provide examples of how you've tackled complex problems or led teams.
  • Be Authentic: In your interview, be yourself. Mercedes-Benz values candidates who are innovative, adaptable, and culturally aligned with their commitment to excellence.
  • Apply Early: Top-tier corporate bursaries are highly competitive. Submitting a polished application early demonstrates organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as a Mercedes-Benz-funded graduate opens the door to an elite career in the automotive and broader engineering sectors:

  • Graduate Programs: Access to structured graduate programs with rotations across manufacturing, supply chain, quality assurance, and corporate functions.
  • Global Mobility: As part of the global Mercedes-Benz network, high-performing employees may have opportunities for international exposure or transfers.
  • Competitive Salaries: Mercedes-Benz SA offers market-leading starting salaries for graduates, with performance-based bonuses and excellent corporate benefits.
  • Continuous Learning: Access to internal training, specialized technical courses, and leadership development programs to accelerate your career growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What fields does the Mercedes-Benz bursary fund? +
Engineering (Mechanical, Mechatronics, Industrial), IT, and Finance, aligned to the needs of premium automotive manufacturing.
Does the Mercedes-Benz bursary include work experience? +
Yes. It typically includes paid vacation work in a world-class manufacturing environment (primarily in East London) and can lead to graduate employment opportunities.
Who can apply for the Mercedes-Benz bursary? +
South African citizens with strong Mathematics and Physical Science results who are studying or intending to study engineering, IT, or finance at a recognized university.
When does the Mercedes-Benz bursary close? +
Applications generally close in the second half of the year (around August). Always confirm the exact date on the official Mercedes-Benz SA careers site.
Can I apply if I am already in my second year of university? +
Yes, current undergraduate students (typically up to their second year) who meet the academic requirements and are studying an approved field can apply.
