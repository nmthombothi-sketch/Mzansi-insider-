About This Bursary

The Ikusasa Student Financial Aid Programme (ISFAP) is a groundbreaking initiative designed to bridge the funding gap for South Africa's "missing middle." These are students whose household income exceeds the NSFAS threshold, yet they still lack the financial resources to afford higher education.

Unlike traditional bursaries that only provide financial aid, ISFAP offers a holistic support model. It combines financial coverage for scarce-skill and priority qualifications with intensive academic tutoring, mentorship, career guidance, and wellness support, drastically improving graduation and employability rates.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Financial Need: Household income must fall into the "missing middle" category (above the NSFAS cutoff, but insufficient to cover university costs).
  • Academic Potential: Strong Matric results or a solid academic record if already at university, demonstrating the ability to succeed in a rigorous programme.
  • Field of Study: Must be accepted into or currently studying a scarce-skill or priority qualification at a partner university.
  • Commitment: Willingness to actively participate in ISFAP's mandatory academic and personal development support programmes.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your application via your university's financial aid office or the ISFAP portal, gather these certified documents:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copies of parents'/guardians' ID documents
  • ✅ Latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration
  • ✅ Proof of household income (e.g., parents' payslips, SARS IT34, or a sworn affidavit if unemployed)
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your financial situation and career goals

What It Covers

ISFAP goes far beyond just paying fees. The programme is designed to ensure you graduate and enter the workforce successfully:

  • Financial Coverage: Substantial support toward tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: Support for accommodation and meals.
  • Study Materials: Allowances for prescribed textbooks and learning materials.
  • Academic Support: Free access to intensive tutoring, supplemental instruction, and academic skills workshops.
  • Holistic Development: Dedicated mentorship, career guidance, financial literacy training, and psychosocial wellness support.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Securing ISFAP funding requires proving both financial need and academic potential:

  • Be Transparent About Finances: Provide accurate, verifiable proof of household income. The "missing middle" assessment relies heavily on this data.
  • Highlight Your Resilience: In your motivational letter, explain how you have managed to pursue your studies despite financial constraints. Show your determination.
  • Embrace the Support Model: Make it clear in your application that you are not just looking for money, but that you are eager to participate in the tutoring, mentoring, and wellness programmes.
  • Apply Early: University financial aid offices process thousands of applications. Submitting yours early ensures it gets full attention before deadlines.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

ISFAP doesn't just fund your degree; it prepares you for the workforce. Graduates benefit from:

  • Higher Graduation Rates: The intensive academic support significantly increases your chances of graduating on time.
  • Scarce-Skill Advantage: By funding priority fields, ISFAP ensures you graduate with a qualification that is in high demand in the South African job market.
  • Professional Readiness: Workshops on financial literacy, CV writing, and interview skills give you a head start in your job search.
  • Alumni Network: Join a growing network of ISFAP graduates who support each other in their professional journeys.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the ISFAP bursary for? +
The 'missing middle' — students whose household income is above the NSFAS threshold but who still cannot afford university fees without financial assistance.
What fields does ISFAP fund? +
ISFAP funds scarce-skill and priority qualifications (such as Accounting, Engineering, Health Sciences, and Education), combined with mentorship, tutoring, and wellness support to improve graduation rates.
How is ISFAP different from NSFAS? +
NSFAS funds lower-income students below a specific government threshold, while ISFAP specifically targets the 'missing middle' who earn too much for NSFAS but too little to self-fund their studies.
When does ISFAP close? +
Applications typically align with the start-of-year academic cycle (around January/February). Always confirm the exact date on the official ISFAP website or through your university's financial aid office.
Do I have to pay the money back? +
ISFAP is primarily a bursary, not a loan. However, specific terms may vary depending on the funding partners involved. Always review the final agreement provided by your university's financial aid office.
