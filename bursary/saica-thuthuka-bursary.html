About This Bursary

The Thuthuka Bursary Fund is a flagship initiative run by the South African Institute of Chartered Accountants (SAICA) to transform the chartered accountancy profession. It funds deserving students at accredited universities through a BCom or BAcc degree, providing extensive academic and personal support designed to carry students all the way to the CA(SA) qualification.

Recognized as one of the most generous and supportive bursaries in the commerce space, Thuthuka doesn't just pay fees; it builds future financial leaders through intensive tutoring, mentorship, and a strong community of peers.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship & Demographics: Must be a South African citizen of African or Coloured descent, aligning with SAICA's transformation mandate.
  • Academic Excellence: Strong Matric results, with a particular emphasis on Pure Mathematics (Mathematical Literacy is not accepted). A minimum of 60-70% in Math is typically expected.
  • Field of Study: Must be studying towards a SAICA-accredited BCom or BAcc degree at one of the designated partner universities.
  • Financial Need: Must demonstrate financial need, as the fund is specifically designed to remove financial barriers for deserving students.
  • Commitment: A genuine dedication to completing the degree and pursuing the CA(SA) qualification.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have clear, certified copies of the following documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration for an eligible degree
  • ✅ Proof of household income (e.g., parents' payslips, SASSA grant proof, or a sworn affidavit if unemployed)
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for accounting and your career goals

What It Covers

The Thuthuka Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to remove financial barriers and provide the academic scaffolding needed to succeed in a rigorous CA(SA) program:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation and meals.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks and a laptop.
  • Academic Support: Access to intensive, specialized tutoring and academic development programs tailored for accounting students.
  • Mentorship: Guidance from SAICA professionals and Thuthuka alumni to help navigate the journey to CA(SA).

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Thuthuka application pool requires demonstrating both academic capability and a genuine drive to succeed in the accounting profession:

  • Highlight Your Math Skills: Since Pure Mathematics is non-negotiable, ensure your math results are prominently displayed and explain any upward trend in your grades.
  • Show Resilience: The CA(SA) path is challenging. Use your motivational letter to share examples of how you've overcome academic or personal obstacles.
  • Demonstrate Community Impact: Thuthuka values well-rounded individuals. Mention any volunteer work, mentoring, or leadership roles in your school or community.
  • Apply Early: Do not wait until the week before the deadline. The portal can get busy, and early applications show organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as a Thuthuka-funded CA(SA) opens the door to an elite, highly respected career in the financial sector:

  • Top-Tier Graduate Programs: Thuthuka graduates are highly sought after by Big Four audit firms, major banks, and leading corporate entities for their articles and graduate programs.
  • Strong Alumni Network: Join a powerful, supportive network of Thuthuka alumni who actively mentor and sponsor the next generation of Black and Coloured CAs(SA).
  • Competitive Salaries: Qualified CAs(SA) command some of the highest starting salaries in South Africa, with rapid progression into financial management, CFO, and executive roles.
  • Global Mobility: The SAICA designation is globally recognized, opening doors to international career opportunities in finance and auditing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who qualifies for the Thuthuka bursary? +
South African citizens of African or Coloured descent with strong Mathematics results who are studying a SAICA-accredited BCom or BAcc degree and have financial need. The fund has a specific transformation mandate.
Does Thuthuka require Pure Maths? +
Yes. A strong mark in Pure Mathematics (not Mathematical Literacy) is essential, as accounting and the CA(SA) route are highly mathematically demanding.
What does the Thuthuka bursary cover? +
Full tuition, accommodation, meals, textbooks, and a laptop allowance, plus intensive academic support, tutoring, and mentorship toward becoming a CA(SA).
When does the Thuthuka bursary close? +
Applications for the 2026 academic year typically close on 31 May 2026. Always confirm the exact date on the official Thuthuka Bursary website, as dates can change yearly.
Can I apply if I am already in my second year of university? +
Yes, current undergraduate students (typically up to their second year) who meet the academic and demographic requirements and are studying an eligible CA(SA)-track degree can apply.
