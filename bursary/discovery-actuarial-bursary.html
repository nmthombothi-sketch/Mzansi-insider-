About This Bursary

The Discovery Actuarial Bursary is one of the most sought-after financial awards for high-performing students pursuing a BSc in Actuarial Science. Given the long and rigorous road to qualifying as an actuary, Discovery pairs full financial coverage with structured support toward the Actuarial Society of South Africa (ASSA) exams.

This bursary not only removes the financial burden of university but also provides paid vacation work, mentorship from qualified actuaries, and a direct pipeline into one of South Africa's most innovative financial-services groups.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Exceptional Matric results, particularly in Mathematics (80% or higher is highly recommended) and Physical Science.
  • Institutional Acceptance: Must be formally accepted or currently registered for a BSc in Actuarial Science (or a closely related quantitative degree) at a recognized South African university.
  • Analytical Aptitude: Demonstrated strong analytical, problem-solving, and logical reasoning skills.
  • Commitment: A genuine commitment to the demanding actuarial qualification path and maintaining high academic standards throughout the degree.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have the following certified documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration for the BSc Actuarial Science program
  • ✅ A detailed CV highlighting academic achievements, math competitions, or leadership roles
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for actuarial science and why you want to join Discovery

What It Covers

The Discovery Actuarial Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to support you through one of the most challenging degrees in the country:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation, plus a monthly living allowance.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks, study guides, and a laptop.
  • Exam Support: Financial and academic support toward the Actuarial Society of South Africa (ASSA) exams, a critical component of qualifying.
  • Practical Experience: Paid vacation work at Discovery, providing real-world industry exposure.
  • Career Pathway: Mentorship from qualified actuaries and a strong potential for a graduate role upon successful completion of your studies.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Discovery actuarial bursary pool requires demonstrating both academic brilliance and genuine passion:

  • Highlight Mathematical Excellence: Don't just list your math mark. Mention any math Olympiads, advanced math courses, or academic awards you've received.
  • Show Commercial Awareness: Discovery is an innovative, behavior-driven financial services company. Show that you understand their business model (e.g., Vitality) and the role actuaries play in it.
  • Prepare for Numerical Tests: The selection process heavily weights numerical and logical reasoning. Practice these types of assessments beforehand.
  • Be Honest About the Commitment: Acknowledge in your motivational letter that you understand the rigor of the ASSA exams and are fully committed to the multi-year qualification journey.
  • Apply Early: Top-tier bursaries are highly competitive. Submitting a polished application early demonstrates organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as a Discovery-funded actuary opens the door to one of the most lucrative and respected professions in the world:

  • Guaranteed Graduate Role: Fulfill your work-back obligation with a structured graduate program at Discovery, rotating through pricing, reserving, or risk management.
  • Accelerated Qualification: Benefit from Discovery's dedicated study support, including paid study leave, exam bonuses, and internal mentoring to help you pass your FASSA exams faster.
  • Exceptional Earning Potential: Qualified actuaries are among the highest-paid professionals in South Africa, with salaries scaling rapidly as you gain experience and pass exams.
  • Global Mobility: The FASSA qualification is internationally recognized, opening doors to actuarial careers in the UK, Australia, North America, and beyond.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Discovery Actuarial Bursary cover? +
It typically covers full tuition, registration, accommodation, and book allowances, plus structured support toward the Actuarial Society of South Africa (ASSA) exams and paid vacation work at Discovery.
What results do I need for an actuarial bursary? +
Exceptional matric results are required, especially in Mathematics (typically 80%+), because actuarial science is one of the most mathematically demanding degrees in the country.
Does the bursary help with actuarial exams? +
Yes. A key feature is structured financial and academic support toward the Actuarial Society of South Africa (ASSA) exams, which is central to qualifying as a Fellow (FASSA).
When does the Discovery Actuarial Bursary close? +
Applications typically close mid-year (around June or July). Always confirm the exact date on the official Discovery careers website.
Can I apply if I am studying a related degree like Mathematical Statistics? +
While the primary focus is BSc Actuarial Science, Discovery occasionally considers exceptional students in closely related quantitative degrees (like Mathematical Statistics or Data Science) who are on a path to actuarial exams. Check the specific year's criteria.
