About This Bursary

The South African Revenue Service (SARS) Bursary is a highly sought-after government funding initiative designed to build capacity in critical fields such as accounting, law, economics, and information technology. By investing in students, SARS ensures a steady pipeline of skilled professionals to support tax administration, compliance, and digital transformation within the public sector.

Beyond covering the full cost of your studies, the bursary offers invaluable practical exposure within a dynamic government environment, often culminating in a structured work-back period that guarantees early career experience and job security.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Strong Matric results, particularly in Mathematics, Accounting, or English. Current university students must maintain a solid academic average (usually 60%+).
  • Field of Study: Must be studying or intending to study Accounting, Law, Economics, Information Technology, or a related field at a recognized South African university.
  • Financial Need: Demonstrated financial need is a key criterion, as the bursary aims to support deserving students who cannot afford higher education.
  • Commitment: Willingness to sign a work-back agreement and serve at SARS for the required period after qualifying.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have clear, certified copies of the following documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration
  • ✅ Proof of household income (e.g., parents' payslips, SASSA grant proof, or a sworn affidavit if unemployed)
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your career goals and why you want to work for SARS

What It Covers

The SARS Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to remove financial barriers and allow you to focus entirely on your academic success:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation, plus meals.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks and essential study equipment.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Practical Experience: Opportunities for vacation work or internships within SARS departments.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the SARS bursary pool requires demonstrating both academic capability and a genuine interest in public service:

  • Highlight Integrity: SARS values ethics and integrity above all. Mention examples of honesty, responsibility, or ethical decision-making in your motivational letter.
  • Show Public Service Motivation: Explain why you want to contribute to South Africa's economy and how working for the revenue service aligns with your career goals.
  • Ensure Document Accuracy: Government applications are strict about compliance. Ensure all documents are certified within the last 3 months and are clearly legible.
  • Apply Early: Government portals can experience high traffic near deadlines. Submitting early shows organization and prevents technical issues.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as a SARS-funded graduate provides a stable and impactful career path:

  • Guaranteed Employment: Fulfill your work-back obligation with a guaranteed position at SARS, providing immediate job security in the public sector.
  • Professional Development: Access to SARS's internal training programs, study support for professional designations (like CA(SA) or CTA), and mentorship from senior officials.
  • Competitive Public Sector Salaries: SARS offers structured, market-related salary scales for graduates, along with government benefits like medical aid and pension contributions.
  • Meaningful Impact: Play a direct role in funding public services and driving South Africa's economic development.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the SARS bursary fund? +
Accounting, law, economics, IT, and data science — fields central to tax administration, compliance, and the modern revenue service.
Is there a work-back obligation? +
Yes. A work-back commitment to serve at SARS after qualifying generally applies, typically at a ratio of one year of work for every year of funding received.
Who qualifies for the SARS bursary? +
South African citizens with strong academic results studying accounting, law, economics, or IT at a recognized university, with demonstrated financial need.
When does the SARS bursary close? +
Applications usually close in the second half of the year (often around September or October). Always confirm the exact date on the official SARS careers site.
Can I apply if I am already in my second year of university? +
Yes, current undergraduate students who meet the academic requirements and are studying an approved field can apply, provided they have not exceeded the maximum funding years.
