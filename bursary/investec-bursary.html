About This Bursary

The Investec Bursary is one of the most sought-after financial awards for high-potential students studying commerce, finance, accounting, and actuarial science. Beyond providing comprehensive financial coverage, Investec offers structured mentorship and paid vacation work within their specialist banking and asset management divisions.

This bursary is designed to identify and nurture future leaders in the financial services sector, giving recipients unparalleled exposure to high-level corporate finance and a direct pipeline into a rewarding career at one of South Africa's most innovative banks.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Exceptional Matric results, particularly in Mathematics (typically 70%+). Current university students must maintain a strong academic average.
  • Field of Study: Studying or intending to study Commerce, Finance, Accounting, Actuarial Science, Economics, or a related quantitative degree at a recognized university.
  • Leadership Potential: Demonstrated leadership skills, extracurricular involvement, and a proactive attitude.
  • Financial Need: While academic merit is the primary filter, demonstrated financial need is often a favorable factor in the final selection.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have the following certified documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration
  • ✅ A detailed CV highlighting academic achievements, leadership roles, and extracurricular activities
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for the financial services industry and why you want to join Investec
  • ✅ Proof of household income (if applying based on financial need)

What It Covers

The Investec Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to remove financial barriers and provide holistic support for your academic and professional development:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks and a brand-new laptop.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Mentorship: Paired with an Investec professional for ongoing career guidance and networking.
  • Practical Experience: Paid vacation work at Investec, providing real-world exposure to specialist banking and asset management.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Investec bursary pool requires demonstrating both academic brilliance and commercial awareness:

  • Show Commercial Awareness: Investec is a specialist bank. Research their recent deals, their focus on high-net-worth individuals, and their asset management arm. Mention this in your motivational letter.
  • Highlight Leadership: Include examples of leadership roles, such as prefect, sports captain, or student society member. Investec values proactive, well-rounded individuals.
  • Prepare for Numerical Tests: The selection process heavily weights numerical and logical reasoning. Practice these types of assessments beforehand.
  • Be Authentic: In your interview, be yourself. Investec has a unique, entrepreneurial culture. They look for candidates who are innovative, resilient, and culturally aligned.
  • Apply Early: Top-tier corporate bursaries are highly competitive. Submitting a polished application early demonstrates organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as an Investec-funded graduate opens the door to an elite career in the financial services sector:

  • Graduate Programs: Access to Investec's prestigious graduate programs, with rotations across specialist banking, corporate finance, and asset management.
  • Professional Qualifications: Full support and funding to complete professional designations like CA(SA), CFA, or CIMA.
  • Competitive Salaries: Investec offers market-leading starting salaries for graduates, with performance-based bonuses and excellent benefits.
  • Global Mobility: As an international specialist bank, Investec offers opportunities for high-performing employees to work in their offices in the UK, Europe, and beyond.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Investec bursary cover? +
The Investec Bursary typically covers full tuition, registration, accommodation, prescribed books, and a laptop, plus structured mentorship and paid vacation work within Investec.
Who qualifies for the Investec bursary? +
South African citizens with excellent academic results and strong Mathematics, who are studying or intending to study commerce, finance, accounting, actuarial science, or a related degree.
Does the Investec bursary include work experience? +
Yes. It includes mandatory vacation work and ongoing mentorship at Investec, which frequently leads to graduate employment opportunities in specialist banking or asset management.
When does the Investec bursary close? +
Applications for 2026 typically close mid-year (around July). Always confirm the exact date on the official Investec careers site, as timelines change yearly.
Can I apply if I am already in my second year of university? +
Yes, current undergraduate students (typically up to their second year) who meet the academic requirements and are studying an approved field can apply.
