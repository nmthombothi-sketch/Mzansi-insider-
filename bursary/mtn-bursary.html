About This Bursary

The MTN Bursary is a flagship corporate initiative designed to build the next generation of digital and engineering talent for Africa. It supports high-potential students studying IT, computer science, engineering, data science, and related commerce fields.

As a leading pan-African telecoms group, MTN is heavily invested in digital transformation, 5G rollout, and fintech innovation. This bursary provides more than just financial aid; it offers recipients structured mentorship, paid vacation work, and a direct pipeline into a dynamic, future-focused corporate environment.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: A minimum 65% academic average is typically required, with particularly strong results in Mathematics and Physical Science (for engineering) or IT/Computer Applications.
  • Field of Study: Must be studying or intending to study IT, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, or a related commerce/finance degree at a recognized South African university.
  • Digital Passion: A genuine interest in technology, innovation, and the future of telecommunications in Africa.
  • Financial Need: While academic merit is the primary filter, demonstrated financial need is often a favorable factor in the final selection.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have the following certified documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration
  • ✅ A detailed CV highlighting academic achievements, tech projects, coding bootcamps, or leadership roles
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for technology and why you want to build a career at MTN
  • ✅ Proof of household income (if applying based on financial need)

What It Covers

The MTN Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to remove financial barriers and provide holistic support for your academic and professional development:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation, plus meals.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks and a brand-new laptop.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Practical Experience: Paid vacation work at MTN, providing real-world exposure to telecoms infrastructure, software development, or corporate finance.
  • Career Pathway: Mentorship from MTN professionals and strong potential for a graduate role upon successful completion of your studies.

How to Apply

Step by step

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the MTN bursary pool requires demonstrating both academic capability and a genuine passion for technology:

  • Show Tech Initiative: Mention any coding projects, hackathons, IT clubs, or self-taught programming languages you've explored. MTN values proactive learners.
  • Understand MTN's Purpose: Research MTN's "Ambition 2025" strategy, their focus on digital inclusion, and their role in connecting Africa. Reference this in your motivational letter.
  • Highlight Problem-Solving: Use your CV and motivational letter to provide examples of how you've tackled complex problems or led teams.
  • Prepare for Assessments: Brush up on basic numerical and logical reasoning tests, as these are commonly used in the shortlisting process.
  • Apply Early: Top-tier corporate bursaries are highly competitive. Submitting a polished application early demonstrates organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as an MTN-funded graduate opens the door to an elite career in the technology and telecommunications sector:

  • Graduate Programs: Access to MTN's structured graduate programs, with rotations across software development, network engineering, data analytics, and corporate finance.
  • Innovation Exposure: Work on real-world projects involving 5G rollout, fintech (like MoMo), cloud computing, and digital transformation.
  • Competitive Salaries: MTN offers market-leading starting salaries for tech and finance graduates, with performance-based bonuses and excellent corporate benefits.
  • Pan-African Mobility: As a leading pan-African group, high-performing employees may have opportunities for cross-border exposure or transfers within the MTN Group.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the MTN bursary fund? +
IT, computer science, engineering, data science, and related commerce fields aligned to the telecoms and digital technology sector.
What average do you need for the MTN bursary? +
A minimum 65% academic average is typically required, along with strong Mathematics results, especially for engineering and computer science degrees.
Does the MTN bursary lead to a job? +
Yes. It often includes paid vacation work and ongoing mentorship, with a strong prospect of graduate employment at MTN after qualifying.
When does the MTN bursary close? +
Applications typically close around 31 May each year. Always confirm the exact date on the official MTN careers site, as timelines can shift.
Can I apply if I am already in my second year of university? +
Yes, current undergraduate students (typically up to their second year) who meet the academic requirements and are studying an approved tech or commerce field can apply.
