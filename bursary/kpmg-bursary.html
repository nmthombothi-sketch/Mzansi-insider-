About This Bursary

The KPMG Bursary is one of the most prestigious financial awards for aspiring Chartered Accountants in South Africa. As a leading Big Four professional services firm, KPMG invests heavily in developing the next generation of financial leaders.

This bursary goes far beyond just covering tuition. It provides a comprehensive support system, including mentorship from qualified CAs(SA), vacation work experience, and a guaranteed SAICA training contract (articles) upon successful completion of your degree, creating a seamless pipeline into a top-tier corporate finance career.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Exceptional Matric results, particularly in Mathematics (typically 70%+) and Accounting. Current university students must maintain a strong academic average.
  • Field of Study: Must be studying or intending to study a SAICA-accredited BCom Accounting or equivalent CA(SA)-track degree at a recognized South African university.
  • Leadership Potential: Demonstrated leadership skills, extracurricular involvement, and a proactive, well-rounded personality.
  • Commitment: A genuine, long-term commitment to qualifying as a CA(SA) and building a career in professional services.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have the following certified documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration for a CA(SA)-track degree
  • ✅ A detailed CV highlighting academic achievements, leadership roles, and extracurricular activities
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for accounting and why you want to start your career at KPMG
  • ✅ Proof of household income (if applying based on financial need)

What It Covers

The KPMG Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to remove financial barriers and provide holistic support for your academic and professional development:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks and a brand-new laptop.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Mentorship: Paired with a qualified CA(SA) at KPMG for ongoing career guidance and networking.
  • Career Pathway: Paid vacation work during your studies and a guaranteed SAICA training contract (articles) upon graduation.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the KPMG bursary pool requires demonstrating both academic brilliance and commercial awareness:

  • Show Commercial Awareness: Research KPMG's recent reports, their focus on ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance), and their role in the South African economy. Mention this in your motivational letter.
  • Highlight Leadership: Include examples of leadership roles, such as prefect, sports captain, or student society member. KPMG values proactive, well-rounded individuals.
  • Prepare for Numerical Tests: The selection process heavily weights numerical and logical reasoning. Practice these types of assessments beforehand.
  • Be Authentic: In your interview, be yourself. KPMG has a collaborative culture. They look for candidates who are innovative, resilient, and culturally aligned.
  • Apply Early: Top-tier corporate bursaries are highly competitive. Submitting a polished application early demonstrates organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as a KPMG-funded CA(SA) opens the door to an elite career in the professional services sector:

  • Structured Graduate Programs: Access to KPMG's prestigious training contract, with rotations across audit, tax, and advisory services.
  • Professional Qualification Support: Full support, study leave, and funding to pass your SAICA board exams (ITC and APC).
  • Competitive Salaries: KPMG offers market-leading starting salaries for trainee accountants, with performance-based bonuses and excellent benefits.
  • Global Mobility: As a global network, KPMG offers opportunities for high-performing employees to work in their offices internationally after qualifying.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the KPMG bursary worth? +
Reports put the KPMG bursary at around R150,000 per year, covering full tuition, accommodation, books, and a living allowance, plus a guaranteed SAICA training contract.
Does the KPMG bursary include articles? +
Yes. It typically includes a SAICA training contract (articles) at KPMG, providing a direct pathway toward the CA(SA) qualification.
Who qualifies for the KPMG bursary? +
South African citizens studying BCom Accounting or a related CA(SA)-track degree at a SAICA-accredited university, with strong Mathematics results and a clear commitment to the CA(SA) path.
When does the KPMG bursary close? +
Applications generally close in the second half of the year (around August). Always confirm the exact date on the official KPMG careers site, as timelines can shift.
Can I apply if I am already in my second year of university? +
Yes, current undergraduate students (typically up to their second year) who meet the academic requirements and are studying an approved CA(SA)-track field can apply.
