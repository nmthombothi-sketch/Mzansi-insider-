About This Bursary

The Eskom Engineering Bursary Programme is a cornerstone initiative designed to develop the next generation of technical leaders for South Africa's energy sector. It supports high-potential students in electrical, mechanical, civil, mechatronics, and chemical engineering disciplines.

More than just financial aid, this bursary combines full funding with structured vacation work at Eskom facilities. This provides recipients with invaluable, hands-on practical experience alongside their academic studies, creating a direct pipeline into the utility upon graduation.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Strong Matric results, particularly in Mathematics and Physical Science. Current university students must maintain a strong academic average (usually 65% or higher).
  • Field of Study: Must be studying or intending to study an accredited engineering degree (BSc Eng or BEng) at a recognized South African university.
  • Financial Need: While academic merit is the primary filter, demonstrated financial need is often a favorable factor in the final selection.
  • Career Intent: A genuine interest in building a long-term career in the power, energy, or broader engineering sector.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have the following certified documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or official university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or current registration for an engineering degree
  • ✅ A detailed CV highlighting academic achievements, math/science projects, and leadership roles
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for engineering and the energy sector
  • ✅ Proof of household income (if applying based on financial need)

What It Covers

The Eskom Engineering Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to remove financial barriers so you can focus entirely on your demanding studies:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks, engineering equipment, and a laptop.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Practical Experience: Paid vacation work at Eskom power stations, transmission facilities, or corporate offices during university holidays.
  • Career Pathway: A direct path to permanent employment within Eskom or its subsidiaries upon successful graduation.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Eskom bursary pool requires a blend of academic excellence and a clear vision for your future:

  • Highlight STEM Achievements: Don't just list your math mark. Mention any science fairs, robotics clubs, math Olympiads, or advanced technical projects you've participated in.
  • Show Passion for Energy: Eskom wants future leaders who care about South Africa's energy future. Mention your interest in renewable energy, grid stability, or infrastructure development.
  • Demonstrate Resilience: Engineering is tough. Use your motivational letter to share an example of how you overcame an academic or personal challenge.
  • Secure Strong References: Choose referees (math/science teachers or university professors) who can specifically attest to your analytical skills and work ethic.
  • Apply Early: Top-tier corporate bursaries are highly competitive. Submitting a polished application early demonstrates organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as an Eskom-funded engineer opens the door to a stable, impactful, and highly rewarding career:

  • Guaranteed Employment: Fulfill your work-back obligation with a structured graduate program, gaining exposure to large-scale, complex engineering projects.
  • Professional Registration: Eskom actively supports and mentors its young engineers through the process of registering as Professional Engineers (PrEng) with ECSA.
  • Competitive Salaries: Eskom offers market-related starting salaries for graduate engineers, with clear paths for progression, performance bonuses, and excellent benefits.
  • Continuous Learning: Access to internal training, specialized technical courses, and opportunities to work on cutting-edge energy transition projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Eskom bursary cover? +
The Eskom Engineering Bursary typically covers full tuition, registration, accommodation, prescribed textbooks, and a monthly living allowance, along with paid vacation work at Eskom.
Which fields does the Eskom bursary support? +
Electrical, mechanical, civil, mechatronics, and chemical engineering disciplines that are critical to the power and energy sector.
Who can apply for the Eskom bursary? +
South African citizens with strong Mathematics and Physical Science results who are studying, or about to study, an eligible engineering degree at an accredited university.
When does the Eskom bursary close? +
Applications for 2026 typically close on 31 July 2026. Always confirm the exact date on the official Eskom careers site, as timelines can shift.
Can I apply if I am already in my second or third year of engineering? +
Yes, current undergraduate engineering students can often apply, provided they are in good academic standing and meet the specific criteria outlined in that year's application. Check the portal for year-of-study limits.
