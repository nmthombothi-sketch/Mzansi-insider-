About This Bursary

The Funza Lushaka Bursary Programme is the South African government's flagship initiative to address the country's chronic shortage of qualified teachers. It funds initial teacher education for students who commit to teaching priority subjects — particularly Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Technology, Accounting, and African languages — at a public school after qualifying.

This multi-year, full-cost bursary not only removes the financial barriers to obtaining a teaching qualification but also guarantees a teaching placement upon graduation, providing a secure and impactful career path in the public education sector.

Criteria Details
Citizenship South African Citizen
Academic Requirement Strong Matric results, especially in priority subjects
Target Qualifications Bachelor of Education (BEd) or Postgraduate Certificate in Education (PGCE)

Work-Back Obligation Yes, 1 year of teaching for every year of funding received

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Strong Matric results, particularly in the subjects you intend to teach (e.g., Mathematics, Physical Science, or a Home Language).
  • Programme Acceptance: Must be accepted or applying for a BEd or PGCE at an accredited South African university, specialising in a priority subject area.
  • Teach-Back Commitment: Willingness to sign a contract committing to teach at a public school for the same number of years you received funding.
  • First-Time Applicants: Priority is often given to first-time university students or those entering the PGCE programme for the first time.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application on the Funza Lushaka portal, ensure you have the following certified documents ready:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Certified copy of your latest academic results (Matric certificate or university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or application for a BEd or PGCE programme
  • ✅ Proof of household income (e.g., parents' payslips, SASSA grant proof, or a sworn affidavit)
  • ✅ A motivational letter explaining your passion for teaching and your chosen priority subject

What It Covers

The Funza Lushaka Bursary is a comprehensive package designed to allow you to focus entirely on your studies and practical teaching experience:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation (or approved private accommodation) and meals.
  • Study Materials: Allowance for prescribed textbooks and learning materials.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Career Pathway: A guaranteed teaching placement at a public school upon successful qualification and completion of your teach-back agreement.

How to Apply

Step by step
1
Apply to University: First, apply and gain acceptance to study a BEd or PGCE at an accredited South African university.
2
Create an Account: Register on the official Funza Lushaka bursary portal (www.funzalushaka.doe.gov.za).
3
Complete the Application: Fill out the online form, ensuring you select a priority subject specialisation.
4
Upload Documents: Attach all required certified documents and your motivational letter.
5
Sign the Agreement: If awarded, carefully review and sign the teach-back service agreement before the academic year begins.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Funza Lushaka application pool requires demonstrating a genuine commitment to education:

  • Highlight Priority Subjects: Clearly state your intended teaching specialisation and explain why you are passionate about teaching that specific subject (especially if it's a STEM field or African language).
  • Showcase Community Involvement: Mention any tutoring, mentoring, or volunteer work you've done with children or in your community. This proves your dedication to education.
  • Understand the Teach-Back: Acknowledge in your motivational letter that you understand and willingly accept the work-back obligation. This shows maturity and commitment.
  • Apply Early: The Funza Lushaka portal can experience high traffic close to the deadline. Submit your application well before the closing date.
  • Keep Copies: Always save a PDF copy of your submitted application and note your reference number for future tracking.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Qualifying as a Funza Lushaka-funded teacher opens the door to a stable, respected, and deeply rewarding career:

  • Guaranteed Placement: The provincial education department will place you in a public school to fulfill your teach-back obligation, providing immediate job security.
  • Structured Salary Progression: Public school teachers in South Africa earn according to the Occupation Specific Dispersion (OSD) salary scale, with predictable annual increments and benefits.
  • Further Specialisation: After completing your teach-back period, you can pursue Honours, Master's, or specialized courses (like School Management or Educational Psychology) to advance your career.
  • Meaningful Impact: Beyond the financial stability, you will play a direct role in shaping the future of South Africa's youth and addressing critical educational inequalities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the work-back commitment for Funza Lushaka? +
Recipients must teach at a public school for the same number of years they received funding. The provincial education department places you in a school after you qualify.
Which subjects does Funza Lushaka prioritise? +
Priority areas typically include Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Technology, Accounting, and African languages, reflecting South Africa's biggest teaching shortages.
What does Funza Lushaka cover? +
Full tuition, accommodation, meals, books, and a living allowance, plus a guaranteed teaching placement once you qualify.
When does Funza Lushaka close? +
Applications for the 2026 academic year typically close in January 2026. Always confirm the exact date on the official Funza Lushaka portal.
Can I apply if I am already in my second year of a BEd? +
Yes, current students can apply, provided they are studying a priority subject, maintaining good academic standing, and have not previously received funding for the same qualification level.
