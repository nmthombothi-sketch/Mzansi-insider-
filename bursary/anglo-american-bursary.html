About This Bursary

The Anglo American Bursary supports students in mining engineering, geology, metallurgy, and related technical fields essential to the mining industry. It covers the full cost of study and provides vacation work and an employment opportunity after graduation, making it a strong pipeline into a well-paid mining career.

As one of the world's largest mining companies, Anglo American offers bursary holders exposure to cutting-edge mining technology, sustainable mining practices, and a global network of professionals. This bursary is particularly valuable for students committed to building a long-term career in the resources sector.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Must be a South African citizen with a valid ID document.
  • Academic Excellence: Strong Mathematics and Physical Science results at the high school level (typically 70%+ aggregate).
  • Field of Study: Studying or intending to study mining engineering, geology, metallurgy, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, or a related technical field at a recognized South African university.
  • Year of Study: Open to Grade 12 learners applying to university, as well as current undergraduate students.
  • Career Intent: A genuine willingness to build a long-term career in the mining and resources sector.
  • Location: While open nationally, preference may be given to candidates from communities near Anglo American operations.

Required Documents Checklist

Before starting your online application, ensure you have the following certified documents ready to upload:

  • ✅ Certified copy of your South African ID document
  • ✅ Latest academic results (Grade 11 final and Grade 12 mid-year, or university transcript)
  • ✅ Proof of university acceptance or application
  • ✅ Detailed CV highlighting academic achievements and extracurricular activities
  • ✅ Motivational letter explaining your interest in the mining industry
  • ✅ Contact details for two academic or professional referees

What It Covers

The Anglo American Bursary is a comprehensive "full-cost" package designed to remove all financial barriers to your education:

  • Financial Coverage: 100% of university tuition and registration fees.
  • Living Expenses: University residence accommodation or approved private accommodation, plus meals.
  • Study Materials: Prescribed textbooks, study guides, and a laptop allowance.
  • Monthly Stipend: A living allowance to cover personal expenses and transport.
  • Practical Experience: Mandatory vacation work at Anglo American operations during university holidays (paid).
  • Career Pathway: A strong employment opportunity upon successful graduation, typically with a work-back agreement.
  • Mentorship: Access to Anglo American professionals and graduate development programs.

The Multi-Stage Selection Process

Because this bursary is highly competitive and offers comprehensive support, the selection process is rigorous:

1
Online Application (Closes ~31 Aug 2026): Submit your application via the Anglo American careers portal with all required documents.
2
Initial Screening (Sept - Oct): HR and technical teams review applications based on academic merit, field of study, and alignment with business needs.
3
Psychometric Assessments: Shortlisted candidates complete cognitive and behavioral assessments online.
4
Panel Interviews (Nov - Dec): Successful candidates are invited for interviews with Anglo American technical experts and HR representatives.
5
Final Selection & Offer (Jan - Feb): Successful candidates receive bursary offers and work-back agreements before the academic year begins.

Expert Tips for a Winning Application

Standing out in the Anglo American bursary pool requires more than just good grades. Here is how to elevate your application:

  • Demonstrate Passion for Mining: Don't just say you want to study engineering. Explain why you're specifically interested in the mining sector and how you see yourself contributing to its future.
  • Highlight Leadership & Teamwork: Anglo American values candidates who can lead and collaborate. Include examples from sports teams, student councils, or community projects.
  • Research the Company: Understand Anglo American's commitment to sustainable mining, safety, and innovation. Reference these values in your motivational letter.
  • Prepare for Technical Questions: Be ready to discuss why you chose your specific field (mining engineering vs. geology, etc.) and what aspects interest you most.
  • Secure Strong References: Choose referees (teachers, professors, or employers) who can speak to your technical aptitude, work ethic, and potential in the mining industry.
  • Apply Early: Don't wait until the deadline. Applications submitted early demonstrate organization and genuine interest.

Life After the Bursary: Career Opportunities

Graduating as an Anglo American bursary holder opens doors to exceptional career opportunities:

  • Graduate Programs: Access to structured graduate development programs with rotations across different mining operations and technical disciplines.
  • Global Exposure: Anglo American operates in multiple countries, offering potential for international career mobility.
  • Competitive Salaries: Mining engineers and geologists at Anglo American earn competitive salaries with excellent benefits packages.
  • Professional Development: Continuous learning opportunities, professional registration support (ECSA, GSSA), and leadership training.
  • Industry Leadership: Many Anglo American bursary alumni progress to senior technical and management positions within the company and the broader mining sector.

Frequently Asked Questions

What fields does the Anglo American bursary fund? +
Mining engineering, geology, metallurgy, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, and related technical disciplines that are critical to the mining sector.
Does the Anglo American bursary include vacation work? +
Yes. It includes mandatory vacation work at Anglo American operations during your studies and typically offers a strong employment opportunity after graduating.
Who can apply for the Anglo American bursary? +
South African citizens with strong Mathematics and Physical Science results who are studying, or intending to study, mining engineering, geology, or a related field at a recognized South African university.
When does the Anglo American bursary close? +
Applications generally close in the second half of the year (around August/September). Always confirm the exact date on the official Anglo American careers site.
What is the work-back obligation? +
Bursary recipients are typically required to work for Anglo American for a period equal to the duration of the bursary support after completing their studies.
Can I apply if I'm already in my second or third year? +
Yes, current undergraduate students in relevant fields can apply, provided they meet the academic requirements and have not yet graduated.
